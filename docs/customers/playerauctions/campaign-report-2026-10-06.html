What the split means

  • Matched: one campaign has a qualifying prior order-ID send, with email agreement required where available.
  • Ambiguous: more than one campaign qualifies; the report does not choose one.
  • Organic / unmatched: no qualifying prior-send match. This includes missing or unusable order references and email disagreements, so it does not prove the review was unsolicited.
  • Both initial invites and reminders can establish an association, including sends before 2026. Test-mode sends and sends after the review are excluded.

Patterns in the validation samples

Delivery, ease and helpful sellers

Higher-rated samples describe quick fulfilment, smooth transfers, helpful sellers and easy tools. Some also contain concerns about verification or costs; a high overall rating does not mean every part of the experience was positive.

Verification and support handling

Lower-rated samples question requests for identity and payment-card documents, describe repeated requests for information, and raise concerns about explanations or support ownership. These are reviewers’ accounts; the samples do not establish whether a verification request was necessary or correctly handled.

Refunds, disputes and seller protection

Lower-rated samples describe disagreements over refunds, cancellations, feedback appeals and delayed seller payouts. Higher-rated samples include a dispute resolved with a refund, alongside suggestions for clearer protection rules. These examples show why topic mentions must not be treated as complaint totals.

Qualitative summaries use three recent higher-rated and three recent lower-rated validation samples per keyword group (or fewer where absent). Samples overlap; they are illustrative, not a full manual sentiment classification.

Definitions & Data Notes

  • Scope: PlayerAuctions project and account (playerauctions); Trustpilot order reviews only. Reviews use event timestamps from Jan 1 00:00 through Oct 6 00:00 exclusive, UTC. No future 2026 dates are represented.
  • Sources: materialized customer-review Delta table, per-campaign production invite Delta tables, and authenticated PlayerAuctions campaign-report API results retrieved October 6. Delta was read with its transaction log; test-mode directories were excluded.
  • Freshness: review source latest timestamp 2026-10-06 12:26:41 UTC; buyer (131) sends through 2026-10-06 12:58:27 UTC; seller (312) through 2026-10-06 12:58:24 UTC. Tickets (427) last recorded a send on 2026-07-02 09:56:09 UTC. Generation-day records are excluded from all period figures.
  • Deduplication: 1,904 materialized rows in the review window contained 1,903 distinct review IDs. One repeated ID had the same date, text and rating and is counted once. All retained reviews have ratings from 1 to 5.
  • Order references are lowercased and trimmed, not guessed or parsed out of free text. The order-only audit found 462 reviews with a prior send match; 13 had multiple campaign matches and 12 available review emails had no agreeing invite email. The final conservative classification requires email agreement when present and leaves multiple-campaign cases separate.
  • The all-review / invite ratio is a period comparison, not causal attribution or cohort conversion. Some reviews can relate to invitations sent before the period, while recent invitees may review later.
  • No recipient names, emails, raw review text, order IDs or private analysis exports are included in this customer-facing report.
  • API engagement covers Sep 7–Oct 5 only, owing to rolling retention. Template coverage was checked against the production logs: buyer (131) uses Invite (309) and Reminder (310); seller (312) uses Invite - Seller (567) and Reminder (310). Their subject lines are How would you rate PlayerAuctions? (274) and A reminder to review PlayerAuctions (280). Tickets (427) has no recent send activity.
