Review Analysis — Praise & Concerns

Every nonblank review body and heading in Jan 1–Oct 5 was read: 125 of 137 reviews contain text (101 Trustpilot, 24 Google). The other 12 Google reviews are rating-only and remain in the informational monthly Google counts; Google reviews do not enter any review rate. Themes count distinct reviews within each topic; a review can name multiple topics, so counts must not be summed. Google’s translated and original versions count once.

112 reviews name at least one praise topic; 10 name a concern; 7 contain both. 10 text reviews are generic or do not name one of the listed topics. Concerns are read from the text regardless of star rating: 8 of the 10 concern-bearing reviews are rated 4–5 stars.

Action Items

Protect the experience customers praise: keep the attentive staff support, careful gift packaging and personalised notes that recur across reviews.

Check delivery exceptions and gift handling: review missing items, failed hand-offs and expectations around delivery speed; keep priced receipts separate from gift boxes. Follow up on the one reported chain failure.

Review the disputed appraisal internally: two low-rated Trustpilot reviews raise credibility concerns, but only one provides specifics. Check the transaction and explanation before drawing conclusions from that allegation.

Track sources separately: use Trustpilot reviews for review rates and headline rating metrics; monitor Google review counts by month for information. Investigate API/log differences and the 13 recent reminder rejects using delivery records before changing campaign settings.

Definitions & Data Notes
