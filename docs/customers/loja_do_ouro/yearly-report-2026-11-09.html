The year in brief. Loja do Ouro collected 148 order reviews on Trustpilot (112) and Google (36) with an average of 4.90 out of 5. Satisfaction is consistently very high: 94% of reviews are five-star and only two are one-star. Customers praise product quality and presentation, fast delivery, carefully prepared packaging with a personal note, and friendly store staff. The year was shaped by two events visible in the data: no invitations were sent between Nov 15, 2025 and Jan 12, 2026 (including all of December, the busiest order month with 493 orders), followed by a large February (713 invites, 34 reviews); and 21 Google reviews dated Jul 8, 2026, which lift July’s review rate to 11.70%.

Key findings

  • Review volume follows invite volume. The two months with the most reviews were February (34, after 713 invitations) and July (31, of which 28 had no linked order, 21 of them Google reviews dated Jul 8); a typical month produces 7–15 reviews.
  • Half of all reviews are organic. 75 of 148 reviews (51%) are not linked to an invited order; most of the Google reviews and many Trustpilot reviews describe visits to the physical stores or contact with staff.
  • Reminders matter. Of the 73 attributed reviews, 45 followed an invitation directly (on average 0.3 days after it) and 28 (38%) followed a reminder (on average 2.8 days after it).
  • Negative feedback is rare and specific. Two one-star reviews (one attributed, with no comment; one organic, about a gold valuation in a store) and seven four-star reviews, mostly about delivery or a product detail.
  • Ratings are stable to slightly rising. Average rating was 4.83 across Nov–Apr (70 reviews) and 4.96 across May–Oct (78 reviews).

2. Methodology — Review Rate Definition Changes in June 2026

Important: the Review Rate basis changes at June 2026, so the two periods are not directly comparable.
  • Nov 2025 – May 2026: attributed reviews only. Review Rate = reviews written on an order that had received a review invitation (invite sent before the review) ÷ invitations sent in the month. Organic reviews are excluded from the rate.
  • From Jun 2026: all reviews. Review Rate = all reviews received in the month (attributed + organic) ÷ invitations sent in the month.
In both cases reminders are not counted in the denominator, and reviews are counted in the month in which they were received, not the month of the invitation. The rate is therefore not same-cohort conversion. Because organic reviews are now included, June–October rates are structurally higher than they would be on the earlier basis. For transparency, the table in section 3 shows attributed and organic reviews separately, and the year-level attributed-only rate is 2.48% (73 / 2,945).

Other definitions: Reviews are unique order reviews (deduplicated by review ID) dated Nov 1, 2025–Oct 31, 2026; no product-level reviews exist in this period. Attributed = the review’s order was sent an invitation before the review was written. Organic = every other review (no order link or no prior invitation), including all Google reviews except one. Average rating = mean of the order ratings of all reviews in the month. Orders are shown for context only and are not used in the rate.

What the monthly pattern shows.
  • Nov 15, 2025 – Jan 12, 2026: no invitations. The last invitation of 2025 was sent on Nov 14, the last reminder on Nov 22, and sending resumed on Jan 13, 2026. The data shows no sends in between; it does not show why. Orders did not pause: December had 493 orders, the highest of the year.

  • Packaging and the personal touch. Reviews describe the gift box, scent and wrapping, and from March 2026 at least eight reviews mention a card or handwritten note (none do before March). One reviewer called the “premium pack” well worth buying (Jun 25).
  • Delivery. Most mentions are of fast delivery (“from one day to the next”, “delivery in 2 days”). The share of reviews mentioning delivery declined from 38% (Nov–Feb) to 25% (Jul–Oct) as service and store feedback grew; this reflects what customers wrote about, not a change in delivery speed.

  • Trust. Reviewers describe the company as serious, honest and transparent, with one noting the in-store official appraiser.

Notable improvements

  • Average rating rose from 4.83 (Nov–Apr) to 4.96 (May–Oct); the only reviews below four stars were in February and April.
  • No three-star or two-star reviews in the 12 months.
  • Presentation became a regular topic: share of reviews mentioning packaging, the gift box or a note went from 11% (5 of 45) in Nov–Feb to 23% (10 of 44) in Mar–Jun and 19% (11 of 59) in Jul–Oct.
  • Delivery issues are rare: five of the 148 reviews mention one (delay and a missing part on Feb 25, a transport-related exchange on Jul 16, the courier failure on Aug 13, and slower-than-hoped delivery on Sep 24).

7. Yearly Trends

  • Stable, very high satisfaction. Every month averages between 4.60 and 5.00; there is no deterioration over the year.
  • Review volume is driven by invitation volume and by external imports. Months with the pause (Dec) or low volume (Nov, Jan) had 2–7 reviews; the post-pause February reached 34.
  • Growing share of organic and store feedback. Organic reviews rose from 22 of 82 (27%) in Nov–May to 53 of 66 (80%) in Jun–Oct; this includes the 21 Google reviews dated Jul 8; Google contributed 36 reviews in total from Feb 26 onward.
  • Review Rate on a consistent basis. Using attributed reviews only for the whole year, the monthly rate ranged from 0.3% to 4.2% and the year total is 2.48%; the all-reviews rate for the whole year is 5.03%.
  • Steady run rate since June. Invitations of 200–369 a month, with the lowest in October.
