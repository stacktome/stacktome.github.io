The year in brief. Loja do Ouro’s review campaign sent 2,945 invitations and 2,848 reminders and collected 112 Trustpilot order reviews with an average of 4.87 out of 5. All performance figures in this report are based on Trustpilot reviews only; the 36 Google reviews, which the campaign does not manage, are analysed separately in section 3B. Satisfaction is consistently very high: 92% of Trustpilot reviews are five-star and only two are one-star. Customers praise product quality and presentation, fast delivery, carefully prepared packaging with a personal note, and friendly service. The year was shaped by one event visible in the data: no invitations were sent between Nov 15, 2025 and Jan 12, 2026 (including all of December, the busiest order month with 493 orders), followed by a large February (713 invites, 32 Trustpilot reviews).

Key findings

  • Review volume follows invite volume. The month with the most Trustpilot reviews was February (32, after 713 invitations); a typical month produces 6–13 reviews.
  • Reminders matter. Of the 72 attributed Trustpilot reviews, 45 followed an invitation directly (on average 0.3 days after it) and 27 (38%) followed a reminder (on average 2.8 days after it).
  • Negative feedback is rare and specific. Two one-star reviews (one attributed, with no further explanation; one organic, about a gold valuation in a store) and seven four-star reviews, mostly about delivery or a product detail.
  • Ratings are stable to slightly rising. Average rating was 4.82 across Nov–Apr (65 reviews) and 4.94 across May–Oct (47 reviews).

Trends and findings

  • Average rating rose from 4.82 (Nov–Apr) to 4.94 (May–Oct); the only reviews below four stars were in February and April.
  • No three-star or two-star Trustpilot reviews in the 12 months.
  • Presentation became a regular topic: the share of reviews mentioning packaging, the gift box or a note went from 12% (5 of 43) in Nov–Feb to 27% (10 of 37) in Mar–Jun and 34% (11 of 32) in Jul–Oct.
  • Delivery issues are rare: five of the 112 Trustpilot reviews mention one (delay and a missing part on Feb 25, a transport-related exchange on Jul 16, the courier failure on Aug 13, and slower-than-hoped delivery on Sep 24).

In-store experience insights

5. Yearly Trends

  • Stable, very high satisfaction. Every month averages between 4.56 and 5.00; there is no deterioration over the year.
  • Review Rate on a consistent basis. Using attributed Trustpilot reviews only for the whole year, the monthly rate ranged from 0.3% to 4.2% and the year total is 2.44%; the all-Trustpilot-reviews rate for the whole year is 3.80%.
  • Steady run rate since June. Invitations of 200–369 a month, with the lowest in October.
