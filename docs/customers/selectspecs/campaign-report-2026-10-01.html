Theme shift, month over month

The lens/prescription spike did not repeat. August's leading theme (41% of negatives) fell to 15% in September, so it looks like a one-month cluster rather than a trend. Delivery returned to first place (48%), alongside customer-service complaints (48%), and frame durability rose (27% from 17%). One delivery detail is new: four reviews cite InPost lockers or parcel-shop collection (a locker that would not open, failed delivery to a locker, a parcel left uncollected in a locker, and a parcel shop delay).

Escalation-worthy

Several September 1★ reviews describe open, unresolved cases at the time of writing: order 4150872, never sent after 5 weeks of chasing and a reply described as rude (Sep 24); order 4122509 (£149), reported as delivered when nothing arrived, with no proof of delivery, paid by PayPal (Sep 7); and order 4158222, delayed from a 10-working-day estimate to Sep 29 with the customer still chasing (Sep 28). At least one more complains that a return request for scratched, apparently ex-display frames is unanswered (Sep 18). As in earlier reports, invites reaching customers whose problem is still live turn unresolved private issues into public 1★ reviews.

Key Findings

Quality improved again on higher volume

Invite volume rose 6.8% and reviews rose 4.6% (327→342). Review Rate (Total) was 5.47% vs 5.60% in August. Average rating increased (4.48★→4.51★), the 5-star share increased to 80.1% and the 1-star share decreased (7.0%→6.1%).

Delivery and customer service now drive the negatives

Of 33 negative reviews, 16 (48%) concern delivery and 16 (48%) concern customer service; the two overlap heavily, usually as a late order followed by unanswered contact. Lens quality, August's top theme, dropped back to 15%. Support data shows that 7 of the 12 traceable negative reviewers have a ticket on file (5 of them within the 30 days before the review), and 64% of negatives cannot be traced at all because they carry no email.

Recommended Actions

  • 1
    Escalate the delivery and responsiveness problems, starting with the open cases. Orders 4150872, 4122509 and 4158222 (and the unanswered return request) are visible in public reviews. Four reviews cite InPost lockers or parcel shops, which is worth raising with the courier set-up. Suppressing invites for orders that are undelivered or have an open complaint (recommended in earlier reports) remains unactioned and still relevant: 10 of the 33 negatives are customers still waiting for or chasing an order.
  • 2
    Review how customer-service contact is handled. 16 of 33 negatives cite unanswered emails, refusals, extra charges or an AI-only first line, and 7 of the 12 traceable negative reviewers have a ticket on file, including 5 with a ticket in the 30 days before the review. The fix sits upstream of the review programme.
  • 3
    Lens quality: no escalation needed now, but keep watching. August's spike (41% of negatives) reverted to 15% in September. The earlier recommendation to raise a possible glazing/QC signal with SelectSpecs can wait for a further month of data. Frame durability complaints (27% of negatives, up from 17%) are worth a look in the same conversation.
