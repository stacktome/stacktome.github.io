Methodology

Jul 1–10 is the prior-month reference window; Aug 1–10 is the current-month window requested for this comparison. Verified invitation conversion rate = reviews received in period ÷ distinct verified-purchase orders receiving an initial invite email in the same period (email_send_type = 'invite', deduplicated by order_id; reminders excluded from the denominator). This is the same metric documented as "review rate (invite-only, distinct orders)" in the Jul 22 report — same formula, new label. Three campaigns send under email_send_type = 'invite' on this account (invite-EN, closed-tickets, positive-reviews); all three are included since all are order-triggered invitations to verified purchasers. Campaign mix was checked and is stable across both windows (invite-EN ≈98% of volume in both), so the rate change below is not a mix-shift artifact.

Sanity check — matched 9-day closed window

To rule out the partial current day distorting the headline, the same comparison was re-run on Jul 1–9 vs Aug 1–9 (9 full days each, excluding Aug 10 entirely): 2,059 invites / 126 reviews / 6.12% for July vs 1,554 invites / 104 reviews / 6.69% for August. Zero reviews landed on Aug 10 in either version — the review table's data for that date has not arrived yet (see freshness note below) — so the 10-day and 9-day readings for August are effectively identical. The improvement is real, not a day-count artifact.

Data Freshness & Window Caveat

The Aug 1–10 window is not closed

This report was generated today, Aug 10, 2026 — the standard rule of excluding the generation day from a window was relaxed here because the request specifically asked for "this month 10 days." The review table's MAX(ts_event) is Aug 9: no Aug 10 reviews have landed yet, and Aug 10's own invite partition is still filling (41 invite events by query time vs 400+ on a typical peak day this window). More importantly, invites sent late in the August window (especially Aug 5–10) have had only 0–5 days to convert, versus 31–40 days for the July cohort by the time of this report. The Jul 22 report documented this account's reviews arriving weeks after the triggering invite — so 6.60% is very likely a floor for August's eventual rate, not a ceiling. Re-measure on a fully closed 10-day window (e.g. once Aug 20 has passed) to get a like-for-like reading.

Data quality note — duplicate raw invite rows (Aug 8)

Raw invite parquet for Aug 8 contains exact-duplicate rows under the invite-EN campaign: 420 invite-type events but only 212 distinct orders — most orders appear twice with an identical order_id and identical ts_event down to the microsecond. This report's denominator already deduplicates via COUNT(DISTINCT order_id), so the rates above are unaffected, but it's worth flagging to engineering — if the app dashboard's "sends" counter does not deduplicate the same way, it will overstate Aug 8 volume by roughly 2x.

Key Findings

Conversion rate improved on a smaller invite base

August converted 6.60% of verified invitations into reviews vs 6.22% in July, even though invite volume fell 27.9% (2,185 → 1,575). This is the same "efficiency, not volume" pattern the Jul 22 report identified between June and July — the account continues to hold a steady-state rate in the 6%+ band across three consecutive measurement windows now (Jul 1–17: 6.26%; Jul 1–10: 6.22%; Aug 1–10: 6.60%).

Provisional reading — likely to climb further

Because the August window is only 0–10 days old at generation time, its 6.60% figure has not had the same maturation time as July's 31–40-day-old cohort. Given this account's documented pattern of late-arriving reviews, the true August conversion rate is more likely to end up above 6.60% than below it once the window fully closes.

Rating quality held steady and healthy

Average rating ticked up slightly (4.43★ → 4.46★) and 1-star share improved (7.4% → 6.7%), with no signs of the quality slippage the Jul 22 report flagged for the back half of July. Sample sizes are modest (104–136 reviews per window), so treat the star-distribution deltas as directional rather than statistically decisive.

Recommended Actions

  • 1
    Re-measure on a fully closed Aug 1–10 window in ~2–3 weeks. Today's 6.60% is a floor, not a final number — confirm whether the gap over July widens once late-window invites finish converting.
  • 2

Exact-duplicate order_id + timestamp pairs suggest a double-send or double-log event on that date; harmless to this report's math (deduplicated) but worth confirming it isn't inflating the app dashboard's raw send count or, worse, actually double-emailing customers.
  • 3
    Adopt 10-day rolling windows as a faster signal, anchored to the 5-6%+ closed-window benchmark. Three consecutive readings (6.26%, 6.22%, 6.60%) now corroborate the steady-state range identified in the Jul 22 report — continue tracking monthly on closed windows for the authoritative figure, using 10-day snapshots like this one only as an early read.
