First report for this project. Wild Earth (outdoor/camping/hiking gear retailer) is newly onboarded to weekly reporting. Zeppelin notebook wild_earth_data_clone created under support_clone. This report covers the full year-to-date window (Jan 1 – Jul 31, 2026) since no prior period baseline exists. Future reports move to the standard weekly cadence.

Data note: the invite event log at this path is Delta-formatted per campaign (not a plain-parquet date glob), and campaign IDs 588 (response-stacktome), 594 (stacktome-review-replies) are review-response campaigns rather than invite campaigns, and 230 (test) is a single test row — all three are excluded from every invite/review-rate figure below. Only campaigns 200 (invite-trustpilot) and 543 (invite-stacktome) are review invites.

Reviews include both service and product ratings — Wild Earth collects order/service reviews (order_rating, mostly Google) alongside per-product reviews (product_rating, mostly Trustpilot/Stacktome/Judge.me) on the same event stream. Every section below reports both.

Email performance API gotcha discovered building this report: the campaign report endpoint (/campaigns/{id}/report) only retains roughly the trailing 30 days of daily stats regardless of the date range requested — campaign 200 (invite-trustpilot, inactive since before that window) returns zero rows even though Zeppelin shows 36,985 invites sent to it earlier in the year. This isn't a bug or a credentials issue; it means YTD email performance can only be reconstructed for whatever is still active within that window. See Email Performance below.

Negative Review Themes

153 reviews rated ≤3★ YTD (150 with review text) analysed by keyword matching against the review heading + text. Categories overlap — a single review can mention both a sizing complaint and a durability failure. Full backlog lives in Zeppelin notebook wild_earth_data_clone.

🔧
Durability / Quality Defects
27 of 150 negative reviews (18%)

Soles peeling off footwear within weeks of light use, zips and seams failing on first or second use, tent/swag seams leaking in rain, straps and clips breaking. The single largest theme, and the one most likely to reflect a genuine product-quality issue rather than a fit/expectation mismatch.

💬
Refunds, Returns & Customer Service
25 of 150 negative reviews (17%)

Orders cancelled without explanation, slow refunds, unanswered emails, and a return policy customers describe as costly (return shipping charged back to them). Includes one high-visibility Trustpilot review alleging a refused ~$1,000 refund over a food-safety concern — see Action Items.

📏
Sizing & Fit
22 of 150 negative reviews (15%)

Footwear and apparel running small or narrow, wrong size shipped versus what was ordered, and helmets/caps described as fitting differently than expected. Concentrated in shoes, jackets, and headwear.

📦
Delivery & Courier
11 of 150 negative reviews (7%)

Aramex named repeatedly for misdelivery to the wrong address and long delays; a handful of "never delivered" / non-arrival complaints. Smaller in volume than on some accounts, but Aramex specifically recurs across multiple independent reviews.

132 of 153 reviews rated ≤3★ (86%) have no matching support ticket — match is by email against we_tickets. This is a large untracked backlog; some may reflect tickets logged under a different email or channel (in-store, phone), but the gap is worth a targeted audit given the volume. See Action Items.

Action Items

  • 🔴
    132 of 153 negative (≤3★) reviews YTD have no matching support ticket (86%) — 73 of the more urgent ≤2★ band (83%). Confirm whether tickets are logged under a different system/email/channel before assuming these are genuinely unactioned; if confirmed, this is a sizeable backlog of unresolved product-quality and refund complaints.
  • 🔴
    Durability/quality defects are the single largest negative theme (27 of 150, 18%) — soles peeling, seams and zips failing early, tent/swag leaks. Recommend a QC review with the affected suppliers/SKUs; the review text names specific products repeatedly (see Zeppelin notebook for the full list).
  • 🟡
    A 1★ Trustpilot review (Jul 5) alleges a refused ~$1,000 refund for 10 bags of dog food over a described food-safety/quality concern, from a self-identified long-time customer. Flagged individually given the financial size and public visibility, regardless of aggregate theme stats — worth an individual follow-up.
  • 🟡
    February had 27 negative (≤2★) reviews — more than double any other month in the window, despite average volume and a still-healthy 4.68★. Worth checking what shipped, or what operational change occurred, around that period.
  • 🟡
    Aramex is named repeatedly in delivery complaints (wrong-address delivery, multi-week delays). Smaller theme by volume (11 of 150) but concentrated on one named carrier across independent reviews — worth a carrier-performance check.
  • 🟢
    invite-stacktome is sending at strong engagement — 56.6% open rate, 2.7% CTR across 16,331 emails in the trailing 30 days. Worth documenting what's driving it (subject line, send timing, audience quality) so it can be replicated if invite-trustpilot is ever reactivated.
  • 🔵
    Two non-invite campaigns exist on the account — 588 (response-stacktome) and 594 (stacktome-review-replies), both review-response rather than review-invite flows — plus a single-row test campaign (230). All three are excluded from invite/review-rate figures above; confirm this scope is correct going forward.
