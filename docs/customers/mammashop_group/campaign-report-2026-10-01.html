A review can carry more than one theme, so occurrences add up to more than the number of reviews.

Bottom line: 52 of 71 negatives (73%) are organic. Campaign-invited customers rate 4.47 on average against 3.85 for organic. Customer-service responsiveness and delivery reliability recur in every month and in every brand, so they are the most direct levers on the public rating. Two items need operations attention: Hoppekids product-spec/quality complaints (bed internal size, colour, CMR/styrene claim, Greenweez marketplace cancellation) and Loukrudt customs/PostNord returns. Separately, review-invite response rates fell as volume grew; the FR campaigns and Naturebaby DK deserve a look at template, timing or audience.
