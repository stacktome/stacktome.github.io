Volume up, response rate down: monthly invite sends grew from 4,757 in May to 9,817 in October, but attributed reviews stayed flat (47 in May, 24 in October), so the review rate fell from 0.99% to 0.24%. Bottom line: 61 of 81 negatives (75%) are organic. Campaign-invited customers rate 4.52 on average against 3.90 for organic. Customer-service responsiveness and delivery reliability recur in every month and in every brand, so they are the most direct levers on the public rating; October added five more late, lost or mis-delivered orders and four CS-handling complaints. Two items need operations attention: Hoppekids product-spec/quality complaints (bed internal size, colour, CMR/styrene claim, Greenweez marketplace cancellation, and in October paint peeling) and Loukrudt customs/PostNord returns. Separately, review-invite response rates fell as volume grew; the FR campaigns and Naturebaby DK deserve a look at template, timing or audience.
