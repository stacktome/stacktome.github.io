Negative Reviews (1–2★)

1★
hoppekids organic
Jul 23 · no order ID (organic)

Dyr børnemadras afgiver problematiske CMR-stoffer

Customer paid 2,799 DKK for a Hoppekids ECO Dream mattress, marketed as Nordic Swan Ecolabel certified, allergy-friendly, and "free from harmful substances." It was not entirely free of harmful substances: after purchase, the Danish Consumer Council's (Forbrugerrådet Tænk) test of the ECO Dream mattress found it released styrene (suspected carcinogen) among other substances, plus compounds classified as harmful to fertility. Hoppekids declined to work toward a resolution, pointing to the mattress meeting the Ecolabel's requirements. For an expensive mattress with such prominent health claims, both the test result and the handling are very disappointing. The reviewer says other parents should read Tænk's chemical test before buying.

Issues: Independent lab test (Forbrugerrådet Tænk) found CMR substances (styrene + fertility-harmful compounds) in a mattress marketed as Ecolabel-certified and "free from harmful substances" · Hoppekids declined to address the finding, citing Ecolabel compliance only. This is a product-safety/compliance matter, not a routine complaint — escalate to compliance/product safety, not standard CS. Search HelpScout/Trustpilot for "ECO Dream" + "CMR" / "Tænk" and check whether other reviews cite the same test.
1★
loukrudt organic
Jul 29 · no order ID (organic)

Man måler sit barns hoved og bestiller…

Customer measured their child's head and ordered the bike helmet size that fit, so all three of them could go cycling on holiday together. Instead, Loukrudt claimed the smaller size was suddenly out of stock and that the customer had ordered the larger size — which is not true, since the customer orders daily goods online and has never experienced this before. They believe Loukrudt has an inventory data error and the order confirmation gets auto-corrected to match. Everything is "AI" at Loukrudt — customer service is AI with auto-generated AI emails, and only once you write "speak to a human" do they make contact; "customer service" otherwise just repeats what the AI wrote. The customer must return the helmet and have it deducted from the refund, and can forget about a bike helmet for the trip. They say they'll never spend money at Loukrudt, Naturebaby, "or whatever else they're called" again.

Issues: Inventory data mismatch — ordered size silently substituted for a larger one, blamed on the customer · Fully AI-generated CS flow with no human contact unless explicitly demanded · Customer explicitly says they'll now avoid Loukrudt and Naturebaby — cross-brand reputational spillover. 3rd negative review in a row for Loukrudt (2 previous were customs/duty; this one is a new root cause). Search HelpScout around Jul 29.
