What changed. Invite volume was almost unchanged (687 vs 694), and the campaign mix was stable: the two onboarding campaigns, Hubspot Main (624) and Welcome Call/Setup (646), sent 70% of invites in October and 69% in September. Reviews fell from 80 to 54, so the Review Rate dropped from 11.53% to 7.86%.

A little over half of that drop (2.0 of the 3.7 points) comes from fewer reviews from customers invited in an earlier month and fewer organic reviews. September received 16 reviews from customers first invited in August, while October received 5 from customers invited in September. The remaining 1.7 points is a real slowdown in how October’s invitees responded: 6.70% of October’s invited customers reviewed within October, against 8.36% for September. On a fixed 7-day window the figures are 6.99% and 8.79%.

Review quality improved. The average rating rose to 4.76★, there were no 1–2★ reviews (4 in September), and only 2 reviews were rated 3★ (5 in September).
Review Rate and email clicks. Email click rate fell through October, from 12.2% of tracked sends in the week of October 6 to 6.4% in the week of October 26, while open rates stayed between 70% and 82%. October invitees also reviewed less often than September’s. Both trends point the same way, and reviews depend on customers clicking through, so a link is plausible. However, the weekly figures do not move together consistently, and September click data is not available for a month-level comparison. This connection is therefore not proven (Section 6). We will monitor engagement this week, and if click rates do not recover, we will consider testing different email templates.

Reminders. 699 customers were reminded in October, against 894 in September. September’s figure was higher because reminders in the first three weeks of the month also went to many customers first invited earlier, including in late August. Customers are still opening the emails, but fewer are clicking through. The click rate halved over the month (12.2% to 6.4%), and the share of openers who clicked fell from 16.9% to 9.0%. Open rates stayed in a 70–82% range. This pattern points to the email content or call to action, or to the audience mix, rather than to deliverability or subject lines. Unsubscribes were low: 7 in total, all in the first two weeks, 5 of them on Welcome Call/Setup.

What the data supports

  • Both measures fell in October. October invitees reviewed less often than September’s (6.99% vs 8.79% within 7 days), and the email click rate halved over October (12.2% to 6.4%).
  • The click decline is in the campaigns where reviews fell most. Hubspot Main and Welcome Call/Setup had the lowest click rates and the weakest review conversion, and Hubspot Main’s review rate halved. The post-course campaigns kept high click rates and strong review rates.
  • A link is plausible. Customers review by clicking through from the email, so fewer clicks would be expected to mean fewer reviews, other things being equal.

What the data does not show

  • The weekly figures do not move together consistently. The week of October 5 had the highest click rate (12.2%) but one of the lower 7-day review rates (6.59%). Late September had click rates (9.1% and 7.5%) similar to late October, yet its invitees reviewed at 9.24% and 7.56%.
  • No month-level click comparison is possible, because September engagement data is no longer available.
  • Other factors also changed. More than half of the Review Rate drop comes from September’s higher number of reviews from earlier invitees (Section 5). Campaign mix also shifted within October, for example Support’s volume fell in the final week.
Conclusion. The data is consistent with weaker click-through contributing to October’s lower review conversion, particularly on the onboarding campaigns, but it does not prove that one caused the other. Because opens held up while clicks fell, the email content and call to action are the most practical thing to test. We will monitor click rate and review conversion this week (November 2–8). If engagement does not recover, we will consider testing different email templates, starting with Hubspot Main (624) and Welcome Call/Setup (646).

8. Key Insights & Recommendations

Key insights

  • Volume was stable; conversion was not. Invites were flat (687 vs 694) and the campaign mix was unchanged, but reviews fell from 80 to 54 and the Review Rate from 11.53% to 7.86%.
  • About half the drop reflects an unusually strong September. September received 16 reviews from August invitees, against 5 in October from September invitees. On a like-for-like basis, the decline is 8.36% to 6.70% (same-month reviews), or 8.79% to 6.99% (within 7 days).
  • The onboarding campaigns drive the decline. Hubspot Main’s review rate halved (8.25% to 3.96%) and Welcome Call/Setup eased (5.52% to 3.94%). Support and the post-course campaigns kept converting well.
  • Clicks fell while opens held. The click rate fell from 12.2% to 6.4% across October, with opens at 70–82%. This is consistent with, but does not prove, an effect on review conversion.
  • Review quality improved. Average rating rose to 4.76★, with no 1–2★ reviews and the September refund and job-guarantee complaints not repeated.
