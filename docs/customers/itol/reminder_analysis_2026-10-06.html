Main findings.
  • 16 reviews were received after the first reminder, alongside 36 received before it (31% of the 52 reviews from this window’s invites). The post-reminder yield is 2.8% of the customers reminded.
  • Support (647) has the strongest post-reminder result: 5 reviews were received after the reminder against 3 before it, a 6.5% post-reminder yield, averaging 4.6★.
  • Welcome Call/Setup (646) has the lowest post-reminder yield of the three larger campaigns: 248 customers reminded, 4 reviews received afterwards (1.6%, and 0.6% once only reminders with a full week to respond are counted). It also has the largest email volume of the campaigns.
  • Post-reminder reviews are rated lower: 4 of 16 were 3★ or below (avg 4.00★), against none of the 36 received before the reminder (avg 4.86★).
  • New students are often asked by two campaigns at once. 125 of the 272 Welcome Call/Setup invites (46%) went to a contact who also received a Hubspot Main invite within 7 days; 112 contacts received emails from two campaigns on the same day.
  • Typical load is 2 review emails per contact, but 1 in 4 receives 4 or more (124 contacts had 4, 12 had 5 or more), which typically means an invite and reminder from each of two campaigns.
  • Unsubscribes are low: 11 across all ITOL review campaigns in the 30 days (5 from Welcome Call/Setup). These are campaign-level counts and are not tied to a specific email.

The same new student is frequently asked twice. Of the 180 Hubspot Main invites, 122 (68%) also had a Welcome Call/Setup invite within 7 days; of the 272 Welcome Call/Setup invites, 125 (46%) had a Hubspot Main invite within 7 days and 18 had a Support invite. In total, 136 contacts received a Hubspot Main email followed by a Welcome Call/Setup email, and 111 received them in the opposite order. These students are asked for a review early in their journey, by two campaigns, each with its own reminder.

Welcome Call/Setup is the campaign where this matters most: its invite-stage rate is only 1.1%, 4 reviews were received after its reminder (averaging 3.75★), and half its audience is also being asked by Hubspot Main. Hubspot Main collects most of the reviews at invite stage (14 reviews, 4.79★), so fewer were received after the reminder (3 reviews, 3.33★). The remaining campaigns rarely overlap with these two (3 contacts or fewer each).

7. Reading Notes

  • Results are observational: reviews are described as received before or after a reminder, not caused by it.
  • Samples are small. Only Welcome Call/Setup, Hubspot Main and Support have more than 50 invites. Treat differences between campaigns as indications, not proof.
  • Reminders sent in the last week have not had a full week to collect reviews, so after-reminder figures will rise slightly. The “7+ days” column removes this effect.
  • Reviews that cannot be linked to an invited customer are not part of this analysis. Review data runs to Oct 5.
  • Contact-level figures count each contact once per email address across all review campaigns, using the initial invite and first reminder only.
