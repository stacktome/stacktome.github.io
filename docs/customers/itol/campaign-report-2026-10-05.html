3. Campaign Breakdown

Welcome Call/Setup (646)
Largest invite source again: 71 invites (up from 57) and 82 reminders, 41% of all invites. Three reviews (4.2% review rate) averaging 3.33★: a 5★, a 4★ and the week’s only 1★ (refund and value complaint). Engagement was the strongest of the large campaigns on opens (76.2%) but clicks were low (3.3%). One unsubscribe.
Hubspot Main Invite (624)
Invites doubled from 32 to 65, with 67 reminders. Six reviews (9.2% review rate, down from 15.6%) averaging 4.67★, with no review below 4★. Reviewers praised the onboarding process, callback speed and named advisers. 70.2% opens and 8.4% clicks.
Support (647)
15 invites (18 last week) and 13 reminders produced two 5★ reviews, a 13.3% review rate up from 5.6%. Clicks were the highest of the large campaigns at 18.5%, though opens were lowest at 59.3%. One unsubscribe.
Other campaign activity
Exam Pass v1 (648) grew from 1 to 8 invites and earned one 5★ review; Exam Pass v2 (649) grew from 1 to 6 invites with 90% opens and no review yet.
