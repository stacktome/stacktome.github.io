3. Campaign Breakdown

Hubspot Main Invite (624)
Invites rose from 50 to 64 (+28%) and learners reminded from 64 to 123 (191 reminder emails, up from 72). Two 5★ reviews (3.1% review rate), one thanking a colleague for a supportive call that built the learner’s confidence on the Business Analyst course, and one praising easygoing learning through quality education and qualified tutors. Opens fell to 71.0% (81.7%) and clicks to 1.8% (4.2%): 154 of the 217 tracked sends were reminders, and reminders open less than invites (invites 50 of 63 sends, 79.4%). Two of the week’s four unsubscribes came from this campaign.
Welcome Call/Setup (646)
Invites rose from 45 to 51 and 66 learners received 66 reminder emails (72 learners and 82 emails last week); every learner received exactly one reminder. One 5★ review (“so far so good”), a 2.0% review rate, down from two reviews last week. Opens were 76.7% (81.1%) and clicks 3.4% (5.5%). One unsubscribe. Twenty of the 51 learners were also invited by Hubspot Main on the same day. The large onboarding campaigns still convert well below the smaller ones.
Welcome Call (824) New
A new invite campaign that sent its first 45 invites on Nov 17, with 25 learners invited by this campaign alone and 20 also invited by Support (14), Hubspot Main (4) or Exam Pass v1 (2). One 5★ review on Nov 18 praising step-by-step guidance and explanations, a 2.2% review rate. Opens were 86.7% and clicks 8.9% on 45 tracked sends. No reminder emails were recorded for this campaign this week.
Support (647)
Invites fell from 32 to 17 (−47%) while learners reminded rose from 40 to 55 (87 reminder emails, up from 46); 32 of the 55 received two reminders this week. Four reviews from 17 invites (23.5%), the best rate of the larger campaigns, averaging 4.75★: a 5★ praising tutor Emmanuel, a 4★ praising tutor Kosi’s availability and weekly video calls, and two 5★ reviews about AI Engineering course content and good support (both from learners also invited by Welcome Call). Opens were 74.4% (71.8%) and clicks 6.7% (5.1%). One unsubscribe.
Other campaign activity

Exam Pass v2 (649) sent 7 invites (4) and earned a 4★ review about great tutors and timely feedback. Assignment Completion (650) sent 2 invites (5) and General Recruitment Support (701) sent 1; neither earned a review. Volumes are too small for rates to be meaningful.
