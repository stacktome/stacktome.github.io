5. Data & Methodology Notes

⚠  Opens/Clicks are not available for May, June, or most of July. The campaign-level reporting API (/campaigns/{id}/report) only retains a rolling ~28 days of daily open/click statistics — as of this report's generation date (Aug 10), the earliest available data is Jul 13. There is no other source: the raw invite event log StackTome ingests from this account records only that an email was sent (and its type — invite, reminder, or response), not whether it was opened or clicked. An account-wide statistics endpoint does retain full history back to March, but it aggregates all 13 campaigns together, including 8 that are not review invitations (Welcome Call, Support, Exam Pass, Assignment Completion, Job Placement, Mock Interview, CV Tailoring/Redraft, Completion/Closure), so it cannot be used as an invite-only substitute. We chose not to publish a misleading number here rather than approximate.

Invites & reviews (Sections 1, 2, 4): sourced from Zeppelin notebook itolrecruit_data (2MN9W4ABP). Invites are deduplicated by unique order ID and restricted to email_send_type = 'invite' (excludes reminders and the Google-review-response campaign). Reviews are deduplicated by order_review_id, since organic reviews carry a NULL order ID and would otherwise collapse together under a naive DISTINCT order_id.

Email engagement (Section 3): sourced from the StackTome app API, summed across the 12 review-invite campaigns for the only window with real data still available.

Desk breakdown (Section 4): built by joining invite/review order IDs to fulfillment_branch on the purchase record for that order. Only form-sourced orders carry this field; HubSpot-sourced orders do not, and are shown as "Unattributed" rather than assigned a guessed desk.
