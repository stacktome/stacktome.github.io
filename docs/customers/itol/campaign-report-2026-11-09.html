What changed. Reviews rose from 7 to 11 and the review rate recovered to 7.97% (4.79% last week), back close to the 8.55% of Oct 19–25. Invites dipped 5.5% to 138 while reminders rose 11.6% to 154. Hubspot Main sent fewer invites (64 to 38) after a busy end of October, and Support recovered from 2 invites last week to 23 (it normally sends close to 30). Completion/Closure was the standout: 3 reviews from just 3 invites, and the two Exam Pass campaigns added 3 more reviews from 17 invites. Customers often review a week or more after being invited, so some of this week’s invites may still convert in next week’s figures.

Email engagement: 293 tracked sends (emails covered by the email platform’s engagement reporting), 223 unique opens (76.1%), 18 unique clicks (6.1%) and no unsubscribes, compared with 280 tracked sends, 70.7% opens, 6.1% clicks and no unsubscribes the previous week. Open rates improved on Hubspot Main (78.5% from 66.4%) and the Exam Pass campaigns, while Support was again the lowest at 63.2%. Click rates remain well below the 11.0% of Oct 12–18. A refreshed email design started a 50/50 test against the current emails on Nov 6, so only about three days of it fall in this week; the volumes are too small to read yet. The latest emails of the week have had less time to collect opens, so the open rate may rise slightly. Reviews are included through Nov 8; a late weekend review may still be added.
