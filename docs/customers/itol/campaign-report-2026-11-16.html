What changed. Reviews rose from 11 to 16 and the review rate climbed to 10.53% (7.97% last week). Invites grew 10.1% to 152: Hubspot Main went from 38 to 50 invites and Support from 23 to 32, while Welcome Call/Setup eased from 56 to 45. Job Placement sent invites again (6, after none last week) and earned a 5★ review. The smaller campaigns produced 9 reviews from 57 invites (15.8%); the two large onboarding campaigns, Welcome Call/Setup and Hubspot Main, produced 5 reviews from 95 invites (5.3%). That is better than last week’s 2.1%, but the gap remains. The average rating fell to 4.31★ because of two 1★ reviews on Hubspot Main on Nov 15. One reports that login details promised within 4 hours had still not arrived three days after sign-up. The other has positive text (“a very good experience”) with a 1★ rating. The other 14 reviews were all 4–5★.
Urgent: one learner without course access. A Hubspot Main reviewer (Nov 15, 1★) says they signed up on Thursday, were promised login details within 4 hours, and still had no access to the course they paid for by Sunday. This reviewer is linked to an invite, so the team can contact them directly as well as reply publicly. See Action Items.
Campaign update: The cooldown was reduced from 30 to 7 days on Nov 13. Following the change, Support sent four invitations that would previously have been blocked by the 30-day cooldown. One recipient left a 5★ review that afternoon. We will continue monitoring the results as more data becomes available.
Email engagement: 381 tracked sends, 309 unique opens (81.1%), 24 unique clicks (6.3%) and no unsubscribes, compared with 293 tracked sends, 76.1% opens and 6.1% clicks the previous week. Invitation opens reached 90.7% (7.9% clicks); reminders reached 74.8% (5.2% clicks). Open rates improved on Welcome Call/Setup (81.1% vs 73.9%), Hubspot Main (81.7% vs 78.5%) and Support (71.8% vs 63.2%). The platform tracked 381 of the 384 email send records; engagement rates use the platform-tracked total. Recent emails may continue to accumulate opens and clicks.

No variant can be called better yet. The refreshed design is ahead on opens for both invites (+6.9 points) and reminders (+6.6 points). The current design is ahead on reminder clicks (7.1% vs 3.4%) and on reviews after a reminder (6 vs 2). None of these differences is statistically significant (Fisher exact test, all p > 0.14); a difference of 4–5 clicks or reviews is still within normal variation at these volumes. The test subject lines show no clear difference either: invite opens 92.0% current vs 89.5% new, reminder opens 76.4% vs 73.4%, clicks within 1–3 points. Each email is assigned a design at random, and the invite and the reminder are assigned separately. Many reminded learners therefore saw both designs, so reviews are credited by step: invite-stage reviews to the invite’s design, and reviews after a reminder to the design of the learner’s first reminder. One learner invited by two campaigns at once (one email of each design) is counted in both invite columns. Engagement figures are unique opens and clicks per email sent, from the email platform; review figures come from the review and invite records.

Support (647)

Three reviews (9.4% review rate): a 5★ review praising tutor Emmanuel’s patience on the AI Engineering programme (from one of the released invites), a short 5★ “very nice experience”, and a 4★ review calling the course well structured, concise and taught by knowledgeable tutors. Opens improved to 71.8% (63.2%) but remain the lowest of the larger campaigns; clicks were 5.1%.
