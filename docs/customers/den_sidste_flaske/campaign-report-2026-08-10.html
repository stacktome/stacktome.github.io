DK's June delivery crisis eased substantially in July. The negative-review rate fell from 26.7% in June to 13.9% in July, and the DK average climbed from 4.04★ to 4.49★. Delivery/logistics complaints are still the single largest theme (roughly half of DK's 34 negatives) — dag-til-dag delivery promises are still being broken on a subset of orders, a driver-language complaint appeared for the first time (courier could not read Danish addresses or speak Danish/English), and one customer described three consecutive failed deliveries — but the volume and severity are both down sharply from June's crisis peak. This reads as a real, if partial, recovery rather than a resolved issue.

DE's attribution rate and volume both declined despite invite volume falling only 20%. Absolute attributed reviews dropped from 31 in June to 18 in July (56.4% → 29.0% of DE's total), a larger drop than invite volume alone explains (1,132 → 909 invites, -19.7%). DE's email open/click data could not be fetched this month — see the credential gap noted below — so it isn't possible yet to tell whether this is a deliverability problem or organic growth outpacing invited growth. Overall DE quality held steady (4.67★ → 4.61★, still healthy). SE continued its recovery: the attributed average that crashed to 3.5★ in June (driven by delivery complainants) is back up to 4.13★, and the Airmee delivery-delay theme flagged in June is still present but smaller (2 of SE's 4 negatives).

Methodology note: June's figures shown throughout this report (here and in the Month-on-Month section) are a corrected recompute of the originally published June report, not a re-fetch of new data. The original June build's purchase-order email-recovery step was not scoped by app_id, which could cross-attribute a review in one market to another market's campaign. Recomputing with the fix applied left DK unchanged (84 attributed) but corrected DE from 32→31 and SE from 6→4 attributed reviews — group-level totals (307 reviews, 70 negatives, 4.16★ overall) are unchanged and match the originally published report exactly, so this is a small, contained correction, not a restatement of June's headline numbers.

Negative Reviews (≤3★) — 42 Total

Complaint Themes

🚚 Delivery Failure — still the top theme, but smaller
~21 reviews
Broken "dag til dag levering" promises, split/partial deliveries, wrong-address drop-offs, and — new this month — a courier who could neither read Danish addresses nor speak Danish or English, requiring the customer to send someone out to guide him. SE's Airmee delivery-delay pattern from June continued at smaller volume (2 cases). Still the largest single theme, but roughly a third of June's volume in absolute terms.
📦 Stock & Fulfilment Errors
~5 reviews
Wrong wines substituted for advertised ones without disclosure ("bait and switch" complaint, DK), only 50% of an order delivered with no ETA on the rest after 14 days (DK), wrong wine style delivered (rosé ordered, red received, DK), and one case in DE of duplicate delivery tied to a confusing double-charge/refund on PayPal.
🍷 Wine Quality
~10 reviews
Mostly taste/preference complaints (wine "not to my taste", inconsistent bottle-to-bottle quality in mixed cases) rather than the acute defect pattern from June. One SE review describes a genuinely undrinkable bottle with a dried-out cork — the same class of quality-control issue as June's clearance-box complaint, but isolated rather than a disclosed batch problem this time.
⚙️ Other
~6 reviews
A satisfaction survey sent before the order had even arrived (two separate DK complaints about this timing), a misleading "sale" price claim in DE (discount computed against an inflated reference price), aggressive post-purchase marketing (DK), and an address-lookup failure at checkout for a long-time customer (DK).

Action Items

  • 1
    Keep pressure on DK delivery reliability — improving, not solved. The negative rate roughly halved from June, but delivery/logistics is still the largest complaint theme (~21 of 42 negatives group-wide, most in DK), and a new failure mode appeared this month: a courier unable to read Danish addresses or communicate in Danish/English, forcing the customer to physically guide him. Whatever corrective action was taken after the June report appears to be working — continue it, and specifically flag the language/address-literacy issue to the carrier as a new, distinct problem from the June "dag til dag" timing issue.
  • 2
    Investigate DE's attributed-review decline and restore DE API credentials. DE's attributed reviews fell from 31 to 18 (56.4% → 29.0% of DE's total) on a 19.7% invite-volume decline — the drop in attribution outpaces the drop in sends. This report could not fetch DE's email open/click data to check whether deliverability degraded: stacktome-app/.den_sidste_flaske_de-pass is byte-identical to the DK pass file (same username/password) and DE's actual campaign (585) returns 404 under that token. There is currently no known-good DE API credential. Get a correct DE login provisioned, then re-run the email-performance check for campaign 585 before drawing conclusions about deliverability vs. organic growth.
  • 3
    Continue monitoring Airmee (SE) delivery delays. Two of SE's four July negatives are Airmee-related delivery delays, continuing the pattern flagged in the June report. SE's attributed average recovered sharply this month (3.5★ → 4.13★) as the volume of delivery complainants among invited customers dropped, but the underlying carrier issue has not visibly changed — worth a direct check-in with Airmee if this persists into August.
  • 4
    Confirm the new DE/SE "invite-tp" pilot campaigns (518, 519). Both appeared in July with 2 invites each — trivial volume, but they share a name with DK's main campaign and weren't present in June. Confirm with the account team whether this is an intentional pilot before it scales, so future reports can label it correctly rather than lumping it into "invite-recent" totals.
  • 5
    Fix the DK/SE email-performance reconciliation gap before trusting open/click rates at face value. API-reported "sent" for July doesn't cleanly match Zeppelin invite counts for either market with a working credential: DK API sent 7,679 vs. 6,200 Zeppelin invites (+23.9%); SE API sent 1,448 vs. 1,037 Zeppelin invites (+39.6%). This is a larger gap than the ~6–8% bucketing mismatch documented for other accounts and doesn't match a simple invite+reminder sum either — worth a closer look at what the campaign-report endpoint is actually bucketing for this account before the open/click percentages are used in a customer-facing context. Zeppelin remains the authoritative source for send volume in this report.
