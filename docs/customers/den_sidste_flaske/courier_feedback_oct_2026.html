How to read this. A complaint count is not a count of courier faults. Each case is classed by what the review says and what the shipping record shows. Reviewer describes courier handling is the customer's own account and has not been confirmed with the courier. Arose before shipment means the problem happened before the parcel reached the courier (late shipment, hand-over or address data); where the evidence is indirect, the report says probably. Cause not determinable means the parcel was shipped promptly and only the courier's delivery scans can show what happened. Markets are not compared: customers in each market review at different rates and use different couriers.

Cases marked * are explained in the methodology note: the customer had placed another order before writing the review, so the review may describe that order.

Methodology

  • Complaints. All 673 October reviews (Trustpilot and Google) were screened. Every review rated 3★ or below was read in full; 4–5★ reviews were keyword-screened for delivery problems. A complaint is a review describing a problem with transport, hand-over or delivery; product quality, vouchers and refunds are excluded. Quotes are paraphrased and customer details omitted.
  • Couriers. Identified from each order's Shopify shipping line (the "Delivery method" shown in Shopify Orders) and, where a fulfilment record exists, its tracking company. Complaints were matched to orders through the order reference on the review. A courier is assigned only where the order record identifies it; it is never assumed from the market. Return shipments are not attributed to the outbound courier.
  • Verification. Order and shipment dates come from the Shopify order and fulfilment records. "Shipment recorded" is the date the fulfilment was registered, not a delivery date. Delivery dates and scans are not available to us and would need to come from the couriers.
  • Later orders (*). Reviews arrive about four weeks after ordering (median 28 days), and for 17 of the 37 cases verified to an order (store pick-up excluded) the customer had placed another order before reviewing. The review may then describe that order, which is listed under the case. Where the other order used a different courier (492734DSF, 497944DSF), the case is shown as courier not confirmed.
  • Not included. Store pick-up, couriers with no complaints, and complaint rates: reviews cover only a small share of orders, so counts per courier and market are not comparable.
