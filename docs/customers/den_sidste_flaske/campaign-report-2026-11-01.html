Review volume nearly doubled on a larger sending programme. Reviews rose from 348 in September to 673 (DK 420 · DE 115 · SE 138) as invites grew 32.6% to 8,919 and reminders 57.8% to 9,210. The review rate rose to 7.55% from 5.17%, with the biggest gains in Sweden (10.43%, from 5.25%) and Denmark (6.77%, from 4.65%). DE and SE invite volumes roughly doubled (DE 608 → 1,396; SE 724 → 1,323), while DK sent 200 invites and 200 reminders every day of the month.

Ratings stayed high: the group average was 4.58★ (September 4.63★) and 79% of reviews were 5★. 614 of 673 reviews (91.2%) are linked to an order that received an invite, up from 57.8% in September (restated on the same rule, see Attribution Split).

Negative reviews rose in number, from 29 to 67, and slightly in share (10.0% from 8.3%). Delivery is again the largest theme (32 of 67, 48%), followed by wine quality (17, 25%) and refund, credit and contact handling (12, 18%). Sweden had the highest negative share (20 of 138, 14.5%) and Germany's rose to 7 of 115 (6.1%) from 1 of 59. Four of the 67 are low ratings attached to positive comments. See Negative Reviews and Action Items.

Unsubscribes more than doubled to 339 (2.19% of tracked sends, from 142 or 1.19%), with Denmark at 262 (2.48%, from 1.31%). Click rates also fell to 3.0% from 7.6%. See Email Performance.

Review Rate (Total) = all reviews (attributed + organic) / invites sent × 100. Review Rate (Attr.) = attributed reviews / invites sent, kept as a secondary reference figure.

Attribution rule (identical in both months): a review is attributed only when the order it reviews itself received a review invite from one of the DSF campaigns (full invite history, same market), and that invite was sent before the review was written. A customer who was invited about a different order is not counted. Under this rule the group attribution rate rose from 57.8% in September to 91.2% in October (DK 50.2% → 86.4%, DE 76.3% → 98.3%, SE 78.9% → 100.0%), and every attributed review follows an invite to the same order. October reviews more often carry their order reference, which allows them to be matched. “Organic” means no invite was found for the reviewed order; it can include customers who were invited about another order. DE and SE organic counts (2 and 0) are very small, so organic averages for those markets are not meaningful.

September restated. The September report counted 229 of 348 reviews (65.8%) as attributed because it also matched on the customer’s email address. 28 of those reviews were about an order that had not itself been invited. Re-applying the stricter same-order rule to both months gives September 201 of 348 (57.8%; DK 126, DE 45, SE 30) and October 614 of 673 (91.2%). Review counts, ratings, invites and review rates are unaffected; only the attributed/organic split and the attributed-only figures change. The 14 October reviews matched only by email address (all DK) are counted as organic. The organic average is 3.88★ on 59 reviews against 4.64★ attributed (September restated: 4.49★ against 4.73★).

Negative Reviews (≤3★) — 67 Total

Negative = rated 3★ or below. 67 of 673 reviews (10.0%): DK 40, DE 7, SE 20. September: 29 of 348 (8.3%). Rated 1–2★: 46 (6.8%; September 22, 6.3%). Themes were assigned by reading every negative review; a review can belong to more than one theme. Four of the 67 (DK 3, SE 1) are 1★ ratings attached to positive comments, which may be rating errors; they stay in all counts.

Complaint Themes

🚚 Delivery Failure — still the largest theme
32 reviews (48%)
Parcels arriving a week or more late (DK 16, DE 5, SE 11 reviews), deliveries that never arrived (DE: 12 bottles with four unanswered complaints), cases left on the public road and stolen (DK), a courier who kept missing the promised dates (SE), a late-evening attempt at 22:45 (SE, twice), and a refused address correction (SE). September: 13 of 29 (45%).
📦 Stock & Fulfilment Errors
6 reviews (9%)
Short deliveries (30 bottles paid for, 24 arrived in DE; 2 cases ordered, 1 delivered in DK; an order halved on delivery), a 24-bottle order held back because some wines were out of stock without notice, and a wooden gift box that did not fit the bottles. September: 2.
🍷 Wine Quality
17 reviews (25%)
Corks that crumbled or broke on opening (SE 3, DK 1), wines described as undrinkable, mouldy-tasting or flavourless, 24 cava rosé bottles produced more than five years earlier, and a box of reds the SE customer says tasted poor and could not be returned. Spans all three markets. September: 12 of 29 (41%).
⚙️ Other
25 reviews (37%)
Refund, credit and contact handling (12): vouchers of 150–250 DKK that could not be applied, a refund kept as credit, unanswered emails, a return refused. Low ratings attached to positive comments (4). Website, email and value points (9): a broken feedback link, Danish offers sent to Swedish customers, and a box description that did not match its contents. Categories overlap with the themes above, so counts add to more than 67.

Action Items

  • 1
    Delivery/logistics is still the top complaint driver (32 of 67, 48%) — keep the carrier review open and widen it. The pattern is late or missed delivery slots (DK, SE), parcels left on the street (DK), late-evening attempts and drivers unable to use door codes (SE), and non-delivery in DE (12 bottles, plus 24 of 30 arriving). Check promised delivery windows against real carrier performance in each market, and how address corrections are passed to the courier (two SE reviews).
  • 2
    Wine quality: 17 reviews (25%) — check cork closures and stock age. Corks that crumbled or broke on opening feature in 4 reviews (SE 3, DK 1), and one DK customer received 24 cava rosé bottles produced more than five years ago. Review the supplier lots and storage behind the affected products and the descriptions used in mixed boxes (one SE reviewer noted the box description named wines the box did not contain).
  • 3
    Follow up individually on refund, credit and contact cases (12 reviews). Examples: a 250 DKK credit that could not be applied (DK, Oct 4), a promised secret gift missing from a new order (DK, Oct 16), a returned case refunded as credit only (DK, Oct 24), 12 DE bottles not received after four written complaints (DE, Oct 27) and a mixed case SE refused to take back (SE, Oct 27). Visible, fast responses would likely convert some of these.
  • 4
    Watch email frequency and engagement. Unsubscribes more than doubled to 339 (2.19% of tracked sends against 1.19%), DK reaching 262 (2.48%). Click rates fell from 7.6% to 3.0% overall. DK now sends 200 invites and 200 reminders daily; consider reviewing reminder timing and content for DK and DE, where reminder engagement fell most (DK reminder clicks 2.7% against 10.1%).
  • 5
    Four low ratings sit on positive comments. DK (3) and SE (1) reviewers gave 1★ while praising delivery, wine or the service; they may have chosen the wrong star count. They remain in the figures; consider replying publicly to invite those customers to update their rating.
  • 6
    Sending and review growth are strong — protect the base. Reviews are up 93% and the review rate is 7.55%, with 91% of reviews linked to an invite for the same order. Keep DE and SE at their October sending levels (invites roughly doubled), and keep watching the negative share, which edged up to 10.0% as volume grew.
