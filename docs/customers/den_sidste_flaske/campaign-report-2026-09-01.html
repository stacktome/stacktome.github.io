Negative Reviews (≤3★) — 34 Total

Complaint Themes

🚚 Delivery Failure — still the dominant theme
18 reviews (53%)
Broken "dag til dag levering" promises, split/partial deliveries with items missing, wrong-address and pickup-point redirects instead of home delivery, repeated reschedules from the carrier, and delivery instructions being ignored. Almost entirely DK this month, plus one DE non-delivery case (order from Jul 1 never arrived). Consistent with every month reported so far for this account.
📦 Stock & Fulfilment Errors
3 reviews (9%)
A customer received six 187ml miniature bottles instead of the full-size bottles expected (DK), a stockout substitution handled with no proactive communication (DK), and an order of 12 bottles arriving with only 6 and no explanation (DK).
🍷 Wine Quality
5 reviews (15%)
Spans all three markets this month: undrinkable rosé bottles (DK, SE), a wine described as tasting "spirity" and lacking character (DE customer, DK order), and dried-out corks making bottles impossible to open (SE). No single batch or product is named repeatedly, so this reads as scattered quality-control variance rather than one bad shipment.
⚙️ Other
8 reviews (24%)
Notably includes a GDPR/consent complaint (DK customer re-subscribed to marketing email without consent after previously unsubscribing, threatening to report to the Danish Data Protection Agency and Consumer Ombudsman — see Action Items). Also: a misleading product-rating/price claim, a 3-week refund delay, a dismissive customer-service response to a pricing question, and a few generic low-signal reviews with no specific complaint.

Action Items

  • 1
    Delivery/logistics remains the dominant complaint driver — escalate the carrier review. 18 of 34 negatives (53%) group-wide, almost entirely in DK: broken "dag til dag" delivery promises, split/partial shipments with missing items, wrong-address and pickup-point redirects instead of paid-for home delivery, and delivery instructions being ignored. This is the same top theme in every month reported for this account — the underlying carrier/logistics issue does not appear to be resolving.
  • 2
    New GDPR/consent complaint (DK, Aug 2) — recommend immediate review. A customer reports being re-subscribed to marketing email without consent after previously unsubscribing years ago, and explicitly states intent to report the company to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet) and Consumer Ombudsman. Given this account's prior GDPR data-deletion request (logged May 2026), recommend confirming the unsubscribe/consent-list handling is compliant before this becomes a second formal complaint.
  • 3
    Wine quality complaints this month span all three markets. Dried-out corks and an undrinkable bottle in SE, inconsistent rosé quality in DK, and a wine described as tasting "spirity" and characterless in a DE-market order. No single batch is named repeatedly, so this looks like scattered QC variance rather than one bad shipment — but worth a supplier check given the geographic spread.
  • 4
    DE and SE review volume is too low this month to read the attribution-rate and rating swings as a trend. DE (13 reviews) and SE (14 reviews) both saw their attribution rate rise sharply (DE 29.0%→46.2%, SE 27.6%→50.0%) and SE's attributed average dip (4.13★→3.86★) — on bases this small, a single review can move the percentage several points. Treat as indicative only; re-check once volume normalizes.
  • 5
    Email engagement remains healthy where volume exists, though the picture is mixed versus July. DK (64.9% open / 9.8% CTR) and SE (63.6% open / 12.3% CTR) both improved slightly on July (61.5% / 9.7% and 58.3% / 11.8% respectively). DE declined (54.1% open / 9.0% CTR, down from July's 55.4% / 10.9%).
