Invite sending recovered in September and the group rating rebounded. Invites rose from 499 in August to 6,727 (DK 5,395 · DE 608 · SE 724), about 83% of July's 8,146. Reviews doubled to 348, the group average rose from 4.26★ to 4.63★, and the share of negative reviews (≤3★) fell from 19.5% to 8.3% (29 of 348). DK carries the volume (251 reviews, 4.60★).

The September review rate of 5.17% is the first measured on normal sending volume since July (group 4.12%). August's 34.87% was inflated by the temporary invite shortfall and should not be used as a benchmark.

Complaints are fewer but the mix shifted. Delivery remains the largest theme (13 of 29, 45%), now closely followed by wine quality (12 of 29, 41%, up from 5 of 34 in August). Six reviews cite slow refunds or unanswered contact. See Negative Reviews and Action Items.

Complaint Themes

🚚 Delivery Failure — still the largest theme
13 reviews (45%)
Missed delivery dates despite "1–2 day" promises (DK reviewers report delays of a week or more), an express service that still shipped days later, a parcel that never arrived, a carrier-returned order after a failed delivery attempt (DE), and a delivery notification that said "in 1 minute" for over an hour (SE). One Swedish-language reviewer reports 6 of 12 bottles still missing after 21 days.
📦 Stock & Fulfilment Errors
2 reviews (7%)
A DK order of 12 bottles arrived without a single label, with the customer saying the listing photo showed a different bottle, and a 12-bottle order that arrived with only 6.
🍷 Wine Quality — up sharply
12 reviews (41%)
Wines described as sour or undrinkable (white, red and rosé), older vintages (2013 and 2014) in a mixed box, and mixed-case quality below expectation. Spans DK and SE, with no single product named repeatedly. August had 5 such reviews.
⚙️ Other
9 reviews (31%)
Slow refunds and unanswered contact (6 reviews; a refund that took a month and 8 emails, returned bottles with no reply, a promised replacement that never came), too many marketing emails (1), a discount code that would not work (1), and a 1★ rating attached to a positive comment (1). Categories overlap, so counts add to more than 29.

Action Items

  • 1
    Delivery/logistics is still the top complaint driver (13 of 29, 45%) — keep the carrier review open. The pattern is missed delivery promises: DK reviewers report orders a week or more late against "1–2 day" delivery, an express upgrade that still shipped days later, and delivery notifications that did not match reality (SE). One SE-language review reports half of a 12-bottle order still missing after 21 days. The share is lower than August's 53% but the underlying carrier issue has not gone away.
  • 2
    Wine quality complaints rose to 12 reviews (41%, from 5 in August) — recommend a supplier/QC check. Sour or undrinkable whites, reds and rosés, 2013/2014 vintages in a mixed box, and a 12-bottle order delivered without labels while the offer photo showed a different bottle. Check the product photos and descriptions in current offers, and the stock behind the mixed cases.
  • 3
    Follow up individually on unanswered refund and return cases (6 reviews). Examples: a refund that took a month and 8 emails (DK, Sep 9), six returned bottles via DAO with no reply (DK, Sep 21), a promised replacement bottle that never arrived (DK, Sep 28), and a DE customer expecting a credit after a returned parcel (Sep 30). Fast, visible responses here would likely convert some of these.
  • 4
    Watch email frequency. One DK reviewer (Sep 27) says they are "bombarded with emails". Sending volume is back to normal and reminders now run alongside a full invite flow; unsubscribe rates are steady (DK 124 of 9,477 sends, 1.3%, vs 1.3% in August), but given August's consent complaint, confirm that unsubscribed customers are being suppressed from all sends.
  • 5
    Sending recovery confirmed — keep monitoring DE and SE. September invites are 83% of July's volume (DK 87%, DE 67%, SE 70%). If DE and SE are meant to run at July levels, they are roughly 30% short and worth a check of their audience segments.
  • 6
    Email engagement is healthy. 58.7% open and 7.6% click across 11,920 tracked sends. Reminders held their August engagement in DK (65.4% open) and DE (54.4%), while SE reminder clicks dipped (8.5% vs 13.1%). First-touch invites open at 45–60% depending on market, with DE lowest (45.1%).
