Overview. Chanty sent 165 new review invitations in the last 30 days. Invitation volume was lower overall than in the preceding period, primarily because the US campaign's initial outreach to existing customers was completed. In Denmark/EU, new invitations actually increased from 136 to 159 (+17%).

Email engagement remained encouraging, with a 52.4% open rate and a 5.9% click rate. The main factor limiting new invitation volume appears to be the number of qualifying orders, especially in the US.

Email sending counts cover all 30 days. Delivery and engagement data are available for October 19 – November 16 (29 days), so they use a different reporting window. Review outcomes are not shown because review records for this period are incomplete.

5. What Is Limiting Invitation Volume?

To understand whether sending volume is constrained by the campaigns or by customer activity, we compared recent orders with invitations reaching eligible customers.

The shipping-date cohort groups orders by when they became due an invitation. The "invitations sent" row counts all sends in the calendar reporting period, including orders due earlier; the figures therefore differ.

Denmark/EU: Invitation coverage was around 89% of eligible orders in the due-date cohort. The main change has been order volume: activity was quieter from late October to early November, before recovering in November.

US: The store recorded 14 orders over the period, with only 10 orders entering the invitation-eligibility cohort. Six of the 14 newly placed orders did not yet have a shipping date recorded, which can delay eligibility. The previous 30-day period included the US campaign's initial outreach to past customers, making the comparison unusually high.

Campaign capacity: Recorded peaks were below daily send limits (76 emails vs. a 100-email Denmark/EU limit, and 3 vs. a 50-email US limit). The available evidence does not indicate that daily sending caps are the main constraint.

6. Key Findings & Next Steps

  • Invitation volume: 165 new invitations versus 295 in the previous 30 days. Denmark/EU rose by 17%; the overall drop reflects much lower US outreach following its initial launch.
  • Email engagement: 93.4% delivery, 52.4% opens and 5.9% clicks in the available delivery-platform reporting window.
  • Main opportunity: Continue monitoring order and invitation volume in Denmark/EU as November activity increases.
  • US opportunity: Review the order-to-shipment data flow and whether additional eligible customers can be reached through existing follow-up campaigns.
  • Review results: Add review count and review rate once Trustpilot review data for the period is complete.
