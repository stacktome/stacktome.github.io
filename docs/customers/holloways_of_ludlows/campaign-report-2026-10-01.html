6. Negative Review Themes

5 order reviews rated ≤3★ in September (all 1★), 5 in August — very small samples, so treat percentages as indicative only. Categories overlap, so they add to more than 5. Themes were assigned by reading each review.

📦
Delivery & Dispatch Delays
Sep: 2 of 5 (40%) · Aug: 4 of 5 (80%)

A stool order still undelivered after repeated promises (now expected to ship from Denmark on Sep 25, with no one telling the customer), and an August order showing “in stock” online but never dispatched. Smaller share than August, but the same underlying pattern of waits against shorter quoted windows.

💬
Unresponsive Communication
Sep: 4 of 5 (80%) · Aug: 3 of 5 (60%)

Now the main theme. Customers report emails and questions going unanswered (twice asking about a product; repeated emails said not to have been received; no reply from the service department), and having to chase for updates on the day of a promised delivery.

📦
Wrong, Damaged, or Missing Items
Sep: 1 of 5 (20%) · Aug: 1 of 5 (20%)

One customer never received the whole of their order.

💰
Refunds & Returns
Sep: 1 of 5 (20%) · Aug: 1 of 5 (20%)

One customer says they will ask for a refund and claim fraud if they get no answer. Both months’ samples are too small to read a trend.

📱
Tracking App & Review Requests
Sep: 1 of 5 (20%) · Aug: not seen

New this month: a customer was directed to a third-party tracking app for delivery information, felt their data was being harvested, and says they were then “hassled by emails” asking for a Trustpilot review after giving feedback directly.

Ticket matching is limited by missing emails. Only 2 of the 5 negative reviews carry an email, and both have a support ticket on file. The other 3 have no email, so their support status is unknown rather than “no ticket”. In August none of the negative reviews had an email, so none could be matched.

7. Action Items

  • 🟢
    Product reviews rose to 27 at 5.00★, all tied to the campaign that generated them, with a 100% product-match check (27 of 27). invite-product is now the largest campaign by invites (1,719) and is producing a steady flow of product feedback. Product quality continues to look solid.
  • 🔴
    Unanswered customer contact is now the main negative theme (4 of 5). Five 1★ order reviews this month: unanswered questions, emails said not to have been received, an order that never fully arrived, and a stool delivery that slipped again (the reviewer refers to an Aug 21 review). Worth individual follow-up and a check on how the service inbox is handled. These reviews carry no order numbers, so they would need to be traced by the customers’ review text.
  • 🟡
    Review rate fell to 1.26% (August 1.99%, January–May 4.5–6.7%). Invites grew 35% (2,162 → 2,928) but order reviews fell from 43 to 37, and 18% of invites went to orders another campaign had already invited. Worth reviewing whether the invite-product volume is competing with order-review requests, and the order in which the campaigns reach the same customer.
  • 🟡
    Order-review identifiers returned only intermittently (11 of 37, from Sep 12). They had been missing entirely since late June (0 of 43 in July and August). Please confirm with the platform team what changed on Sep 12 and whether full restoration is expected; until it is, campaign-level attribution and ticket matching for order reviews remain unreliable.
  • 🟡
    Customer-experience details worth a look: one reviewer felt pushed into a third-party tracking app by the delivery email, and says they were pestered for a Trustpilot review after already giving feedback. invite-tp unsubscribes edged up to 2.4% (August 2.1%), and invite-product’s click-through rate (2.3%) remains the weakest. Consider the delivery-email wording and the reminder cadence.
  • 🟢
    Email engagement is healthy: 63.3% open and 3.6% click across 5,079 tracked sends; positive-closed-tickets continues to lead on engagement. positive-closed-tickets volume recovered slightly (28 → 33 invites) but is still below July’s 45.
