7. Action Items

  • 🔴
    All three negative order reviews (3 of 3) are about long waits and having to chase for updates. The cases: wrong cushion design with the replacement still outstanding (Oct 18), 10 days without an update and an unanswered email (Oct 22), and a delivery pushed from early November to mid-December (Oct 30). Each has a support ticket on file. Worth individual follow-up, and a check that long-lead-time orders get proactive status updates rather than customer-initiated chasing.
  • 🔴
    Product reviews softened to 4.39★ with 4 rated ≤3★ after 27 perfect reviews in September. The causes are specific: a pendant that arrived broken with inadequate packaging and a dispute over courier responsibility, a lounge set whose second part was still outstanding after 52 days, a chandelier that did not look like its photos, and a bulb whose light colour differed from its description. Review packaging for fragile paper shades, product photos and descriptions for the chandelier and bulb, and how partial shipments are communicated.
  • 🟡
    Invite volume fell 16.6% (2,928 → 2,442), driven by invite-product (1,719 → 1,144). Order reviews fell from 37 to 32 and the review rate edged up to 1.31% (September 1.26%; January–May 4.5–6.7%). 17% of invites went to orders another campaign had already invited. Worth reviewing whether invite-product volume is competing with order-review requests, and the order in which the campaigns reach the same customer.
  • 🟡
    Attribution Rate was flat at 28.1% (9 of 32 order reviews; September 27.8%). Only 13 of 32 order reviews (41%) carry an order number, so most reviews cannot be linked to the campaign that prompted them, and the 23 unmatched reviews are not necessarily unprompted. Please confirm whether full order numbers are expected on every order review; until then, campaign-level attribution and ticket matching for order reviews remain limited.
  • 🟡
    New recovery campaign (821) started on Oct 23. It sent 5 emails in October (3 follow-ups and 2 reminders; 4 opened, none clicked). Volume is too small to judge; watch whether recovered customers go on to update their reviews.
  • 🟢
    Email engagement is healthy: 65.6% open and 3.7% click across 4,144 tracked sends; positive-closed-tickets continues to lead (80.6% open, 12.9% click). invite-product’s click-through improved to 2.8% but remains the weakest, and unsubscribes edged up to 2.4% of tracked sends (September 2.2%). Consider the reminder cadence on the two large campaigns.
  • 🟢
    Order-level rating improved to 4.59★ (September 4.41★) with 28 of 32 order reviews at 5★; customers praise competitive prices, fast delivery and helpful service when there is an issue.
