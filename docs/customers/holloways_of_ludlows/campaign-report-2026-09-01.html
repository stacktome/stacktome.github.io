5. Product Reviews

22 product reviews in August (avg 4.68★, 3 rated ≤3★), up from 5 in July (avg 4.20★, 1 rated ≤3★). 100% of both months' product reviews are tied to a specific campaign — none are unattributed. 6. Negative Review Themes

5 order reviews rated ≤3★ in August, 9 in July — both small samples, so treat percentages as indicative only. Categories overlap.

📦
Delivery & Dispatch Delays
Aug: 4 of 5 (80%) · Jul: 8 of 9 (89%)

Still the dominant theme by far in both months, consistent with the year-to-date report's 73% baseline. Waits of weeks to months against much shorter quoted windows continue to be the throughline across nearly every negative review.

💬
Unresponsive Communication
Aug: 3 of 5 (60%) · Jul: 5 of 9 (56%)

Customers repeatedly chasing for updates via phone, email, and in one case social media, often with no proactive contact from Holloways throughout a multi-week or multi-month wait.

📦
Wrong, Damaged, or Missing Items
Aug: 1 of 5 (20%) · Jul: 2 of 9 (22%)

August's case: a missing garden stool with no proactive notice, then a wrong replacement sent after a further 5-week wait.

💰
Refunds & Returns
Aug: 1 of 5 (20%) · Jul: 4 of 9 (44%)

Lower share in August than July, though both months' sample sizes (5 and 9) are too small to read a real trend into the drop.

Representative Negative Reviews (August)

1★Appalling experience!

Order placed ~3.5 months ago for external furniture with promised white-glove delivery; the delivery company had no knowledge of that arrangement. After 3 further weeks with no action, the customer had to unpack, assemble, and dispose of packaging themselves. A "goodwill" gift turned out to be an entitlement never disclosed at sale. A garden stool went missing from the shipment with no proactive notice; after a further 5-week wait, the replacement arrived as the wrong item.

1★Order never arrived

Tom Dixon garden furniture ordered without being told it was out of stock. After 6 weeks with no clear updates the customer had to cancel; a full refund was eventually secured, but summer passed without the furniture ever arriving.

1★The standard of service

Order placed 3 Mar, delivered 28 Jul — a 5-month wait with repeated contact attempts and no explanation from customer service. Contrasts sharply with the customer's long-standing positive experience shopping in-store.

2★Had a nightmare with the delivery

Showroom promised a callback on the day that never came; parcel arrived a day late.

3★Very slow delivery

"In stock" shelving advertised as same-day dispatch instead took 10+ days, with customer service repeatedly promising imminent courier pickup that didn't happen.
