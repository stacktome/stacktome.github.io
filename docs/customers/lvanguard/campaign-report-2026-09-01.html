Negative Review Themes

25 reviews rated ≤3★ in August (Carethy 13, Bulevip 12) analysed by theme via keyword matching across the review text (EN/ES/FR/DE/IT/NL/PT), same categories and method as the Jul 22 report. This month's pool (25 reviews) is much smaller than the YTD report's (238 reviews) — treat the percentages below as indicative, not confirmed. Categories overlap — a single review can mention both a delivery failure and an unresponsive support team.

📦
Delivery & Carrier Failures
8 of 25 negative reviews (32%) · Carethy 2, Bulevip 6

Still the dominant theme, consistent with the YTD finding. This month it skews toward Bulevip: a delayed "urgent" delivery in Spain where the carrier disputed the address after the fact, a >7-day shipping estimate driving a lost customer, and a bottle that arrived open in transit due to a mislabelled box.

🔧
Damaged / Wrong / Missing Items
2 of 25 negative reviews (8%) · Carethy 1, Bulevip 1

A Carethy customer received the wrong products entirely ("Ich habe die falschen Produkte erhalten"); a Bulevip customer's multi-item order arrived with items missing and was never refunded for them despite photo evidence being provided twice.

💬
Refund Delays & Unresponsive Support
4 of 25 negative reviews (16%) · Carethy 2, Bulevip 2

Notable this month: two Carethy 1★ reviews cite orders 2–2.5 years old that were never fulfilled or refunded, both explicitly using the word "fraud." These read as long-dormant complaints surfacing now rather than fresh August order failures — distinct in severity from the routine refund-delay complaints in this category and worth escalating separately (see Action Items).

✉️
Missing Order Confirmation / Communication
1 of 25 negative reviews (4%) · Carethy 1, Bulevip 0

One Carethy customer (UK, order placed Aug 12) reports no confirmation email at all and had to screenshot the order as their only proof of purchase. Same low-cost-fix framing as the Jul 22 report if it traces to a transactional-email issue.

Match is by email against lv_tickets; both rates are consistent with the Jul 22 report (80% / 58%), so this looks like a stable pattern rather than a one-month blip. See Action Items.
