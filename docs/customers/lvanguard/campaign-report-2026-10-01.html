Delivery & Carrier Failures
12 of 22 reviews (55%) · Carethy 6, Bulevip 6 · Aug 32%

Still the dominant theme and now clearly larger. Carethy: orders not received after 3.5 weeks (NL), 2 weeks (PL) and stuck in Germany since Sep 8, a Mexico order not received, and two complaints that delivery was too slow. Bulevip: non-delivery, a parcel handed to an unknown person (IT), a carrier-returned order, a pick-up point ignored, and one customer who was asked to rate the experience before the order had arrived.

🔧
Damaged / Wrong / Poor-quality Items
5 of 22 reviews (23%) · Carethy 3, Bulevip 2 · Aug 8%

Carethy: pump heads destroyed in transit, three products leaked and damaged in the box, and a damaged, opened bottle. Bulevip: a product reported as breaking quickly, and a 60 ml bottle sent against 75 ml pictured on the listing.

💬
Refund Delays & Unresponsive Support
5 of 22 reviews (23%) · Carethy 2, Bulevip 3 · Aug 16%

Reviewers who emailed and did not hear back (Bulevip order 309667; a Carethy customer who also reports no refund for a damaged item), a return request unanswered, and a refund after a carrier-returned order expected to take over a week. No fraud allegations this month, unlike August.

🧾
Import Duty / VAT on Delivery
3 of 22 reviews (14%) · Carethy 3 · not tracked in Aug

Three German-language Carethy reviewers (two 1★, one 3★) were charged duty or VAT on delivery, one saying it can reach 25% of the price. One ordered through a German-language page and did not expect the goods to ship from the Netherlands. Worth a check of how duties are shown at checkout for this audience.

12 of 18 reviews rated ≤2★ have no matching support ticket — Carethy 7 of 10 (70%), Bulevip 5 of 8 (63%); August (published): 15 of 21. Tickets are matched by email against lv_tickets. All 12 unmatched reviews carry no email at all (the review isn't linked to an order), and all 6 negative reviewers with an email did have a ticket. So "no ticket" here means "support status unknown", not "ignored by support". The same limitation applied to the August figures.

Action Items

  • 🔴
    Delivery complaints are now 55% of ≤3★ reviews (12 of 22) vs 32% in August. Several are non-arrivals of 2–3.5 weeks, and one Bulevip reviewer (1★, Sep 19) was asked to rate the experience before the order arrived. Consider holding the invite until delivery is confirmed (or a few days after the carrier's delivered scan), and review the carriers involved in Germany, the Netherlands, Poland and Italy.
  • 🟡
    Import duty/VAT surprises on Carethy German-language orders (3 reviews, Sep 4–25). Customers say charges of up to 25% were added on delivery for orders shipped from the Netherlands. Confirm how duties and the ship-from country are shown at checkout for this audience.
  • 🟡
    Unanswered contact or refunds in 5 reviews. Bulevip order 309667 (1★, Sep 28, "sent an email, no reply"), Bulevip order 3015119 (return request, no response), a Bulevip customer waiting on a refund after a carrier-returned order, a Carethy 1★ (Sep 6) reporting a damaged opened bottle with no reply or refund, and a Carethy 1★ (Sep 29) who could not reach anyone about a late order. Escalate individually; those without an email on the review (309667 and the two Carethy cases) cannot be matched to a ticket.
  • 🟡
    Ticket matching is only possible for reviews with an email. 12 of 18 negative reviews have none, so the "no ticket" count (12) shows missing identity, not missing support follow-up. To size genuinely unactioned complaints, link these reviews to orders or capture the reviewer email at review time. This also reframes the August action item about Carethy's "83% untracked" negatives.
  • 🟢
    Carethy's Aug 15–27 invite pause did not recur, and invites ran every day in September on both brands. The cause of the August pause is still not identified in these data, so the open question to the account team stands. Minor dips: Carethy 0 invites on Sep 10; both brands softer on Sep 16–18; Bulevip sent only 8 invites and 7 reminders on Sep 25, with reminders back to normal the next day.
  • 🟢
    Ratings improved: group 4.09★ to 4.24★, Carethy 3.84★ to 4.08★, Bulevip 4.25★ to 4.37★, with negatives at 16.7% (Aug 18.9%). Review rate is 3.08% (Aug 3.31%): Carethy's fell to 3.17% from 3.73% as invites grew 31% against a small rise in reviews; Bulevip held at 3.01% (Aug 3.09%).
  • 🟢
    Email performance is stable: 49.9% open rate, 4.7% CTR across 9,541 tracked sends. Bulevip FR (62.2%), DE (61.4%, 9.0% CTR) and IT (59.7%) outperform the group average, as in August.
  • 🔵
    Housekeeping: lvanguard_biuky remains out of scope. The August baseline is kept as published; Bulevip's August reviews have since changed in the source data (66 reviews at 4.30★ now vs 67 at 4.25★ published).
