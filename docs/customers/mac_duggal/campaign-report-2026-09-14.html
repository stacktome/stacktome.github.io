A handful of older, smaller (1–2 row) duplicates were also found scattered back to December 2025, but the Sep 4–5 and Sep 12–13 instances are an order of magnitude larger (roughly 2× the true count) than anything seen before August. Worth flagging to engineering as a recurring, possibly worsening pattern rather than continuing to note it passively each week.
