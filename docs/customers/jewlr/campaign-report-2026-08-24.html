4. Ticket-Gating Fix — Impact

Segment 1158 (invite-eligible-no-ticket, feeds campaign 735) had its exclusion template fixed on Aug 10 (see the Aug 10 report) — swapping from a status-literal check that never matched Jewlr's ticket data to a null-safe any-status check. This section covers what happened in the two weeks since.

The fix continues to work as intended. Zero of the 342 distinct customers invited this window carried an open (active/pending) support ticket at invite time. One customer (mike2fernandez28@gmail.com) did open a ticket during the window — but not until Aug 23, three days after their Aug 20 invite — so this is not a gating failure. The segment correctly excludes based on ticket status as of invite time — a ticket opened afterward isn't something the pre-invite gate can or should catch.

The segment's post-fix decline has paused, and reversed slightly, for the first time. After dropping from 2,357 (pre-fix) to 1,949 (Aug 10) to 1,196 (Aug 17) — a cumulative 49% contraction over two weeks — the live segment stands at 1,223 as of Aug 24, a net gain of 27 (+2.3%) this week. The change log shows a mixed week that landed net-positive: two notably large single-day additions, Aug 20 (+73) and Aug 21 (+95, the largest single-day intake since the fix landed), outweighed removals on Aug 17–19 and Aug 23. This is the first week since the fix where additions have kept pace with, and modestly exceeded, removals.

This lines up with invite volume itself rebounding to 342 this window (window-average ~49/day) from 170 last week (~30/day, per the prior report). Still too early to call this the new steady state — one week of net growth after a 49% two-week contraction isn't yet a trend — but it's the first sign the post-fix contraction may be stabilizing around new-order inflow rather than continuing to compound. Worth another 1–2 weeks of observation before revisiting whether the any-status exclusion needs narrowing.
