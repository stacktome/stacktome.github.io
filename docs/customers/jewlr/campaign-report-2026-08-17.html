4. Ticket-Gating Fix — Impact

Segment 1158 (invite-eligible-no-ticket, feeds campaign 735) had its exclusion template fixed on Aug 10 (see last week's report) — swapping from a status-literal check that never matched Jewlr's ticket data to a null-safe any-status check. This section covers what happened in the week since.

The fix is working as intended. Of the 170 distinct customers invited this window, zero carried an active or pending support ticket at invite time — a clean result compared to the pre-fix state, where the exclusion was a permanent no-op.

But the fix triggered a much larger volume effect than anticipated. The live segment dropped from 2,357 (pre-fix) to 1,949 right after the fix landed (Aug 10), and has kept shrinking since — it stands at 1,196 as of Aug 17, a further 39% drop in the week following the fix. The segment's own change log shows why: Aug 10 alone removed 98 customers with zero additions and zero invites sent that day — the entire pre-fix backlog of eligible-but-not-yet-invited orders was swept out in one pass. Net removals have outpaced additions on all but one day since.

This has bottlenecked daily invite volume well below the campaign's 100/day cap. With the backlog gone, campaign 735 is now sending almost exactly as many invites as new orders become eligible each day, not the cap: Aug 11 (28 added / 29 invited), Aug 12 (30 / 30), Aug 13 (41 / 41), Aug 14 (42 / 42), Aug 15 (28 / 28). Daily invite volume has settled around 30/day, roughly 30% of the ~100/day run rate through the end of July.

This also resolves last week's open question about the Aug 9 pacing gap: invites paused Aug 9–10 specifically because of this transition, not an isolated scheduling glitch. Open decision for the account owner: is ~30/day the new sustainable baseline (i.e. the ticket exclusion is correctly filtering out a large share of recent orders), or is the any-status exclusion too aggressive and worth narrowing? Either way, the current volume is unlikely to recover on its own unless new-order inflow grows.
