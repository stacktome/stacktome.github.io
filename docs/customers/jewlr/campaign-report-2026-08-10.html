The brand template is losing on every engagement metric, consistently, on both steps. A 14–16 percentage-point open-rate gap this large and this consistent (invite and reminder both down, in the same direction, at similar magnitude) is unusual for a template-content-only change — worth ruling out a deliverability issue specific to the brand template before concluding it's simply a weaker creative: check sender reputation/headers for template 1181/1182 sends, whether the brand template embeds a Jewlr logo image that could be flagged, and whether from-name/from-address differ between the two variants.

Review-conversion favors TP by a similar margin (2.2× on invite, 2.5× on reminder), though sample sizes are still small (7–15 reviews per cell) — the open-rate gap is the more statistically solid signal at this volume. Recommendation: hold the current 50/50 split for one more full week to confirm the pattern isn't a first-week novelty effect, then consider dropping brand-v1's weight (or pausing it) if the open-rate gap persists, rather than waiting on review-conversion alone to decide.

4. Ticket-Gating Fix (landed Aug 10, end of this window)

Segment 1158 (invite-eligible-no-ticket, feeds campaign 735) previously excluded customers only if their ticket status was open or new — literal values that never occur in Jewlr's HelpScout-sourced ticket data (which only ever produces closed, active, pending, or spam). The exclusion had been a permanent no-op since the segment was created.

Fixed today by swapping the segment's exclusion template from 117 (status-literal match) to 435 (any ticket, any status, null-safe) — both pre-existing global templates, so no other account was affected. Verified before/after against the live segment-state table: the customer whose negative review (Section 5) surfaced this bug was confirmed present pre-fix and confirmed removed post-fix. Live segment size dropped 2,357 → 1,949 (408 customers with any-status ticket in the trailing 90 days now correctly excluded).

This landed after the review window closed (Aug 9), so it had zero effect on the numbers above — its impact will show up starting in the next report.
