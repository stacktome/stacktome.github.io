7. Action Items

Prompt case review: reported device safety issues
Verify the ZIIP device-fire report and the adverse-response reports through the existing product-quality and support processes. Confirm the case history, returned-device assessment and resolution. The review text alone does not establish a technical cause.
ZIIP: clarify return and warranty expectations, then reduce repeated troubleshooting
Returns/refunds/warranty appear in 20 of 55 negative reviews; support in 18; device reliability in 17. Review the clarity of opened-product and warranty terms at purchase, and how exceptions and replacements are communicated. Examine recurring charging/power failures and replacement cases alongside the seven negative app/syncing complaints.
AU: improve delivery updates and mask fitting guidance
Support appears in 12 of 30 negative reviews; delivery in 10; fit/usability in 10. Audit delivery estimates and missing-item escalation. Add clear fitting and strap guidance, and route recurring comfort and eye-insert concerns to the product team.
Campaign follow-up: review timing and click-through
Check whether review requests arrive before customers have enough product experience; this issue appears in neutral as well as negative feedback. ZIIP invite - TP (393) had lower click-through than AU invite - TP (288) in the email window. A future controlled test could assess CTA clarity, while keeping audience and send timing comparable.

For complaints that mention support contact, compare invite and ticket timestamps before concluding that ticket exclusion failed. Current AU invites-tp-au (125) uses invites-all-recent (474); ZIIP invite-tp (214) uses invites-all-recent (546). Ticket history was not used to infer state at send in this report.

Methodology and sources

Sources: currentbody_group_data notebook (2MTBPBJBC), new paragraphs dated Oct 7, 2026; the currentbody customer_review Delta table and account-scoped invite event logs; StackTome campaign report API for invites-tp-au (125), invites-tp-au-okendo (134) and invite-tp (214). Review data reaches Oct 7 for AU and Oct 5 for ZIIP; both extend beyond the Sep 30 reporting cutoff. Timestamps use source ts_event in UTC, not ingestion partitions.

Attribution: deduplicate by account and order_review_id, keeping the latest inserted row; match normalized order IDs within the same account to a non-test Trustpilot initial invite sent before the review. If several initial invites match, assign the latest preceding one, once per review. Full available invite history is used for attribution; invite denominators are distinct account/campaign/order combinations sent within the reporting period. No email-only or verification-only match is used. Complaint themes describe current review text, including translated non-English feedback; they are human-coded and may overlap.
