Methodology and Data Coverage

Source: Mattress Online invite-event parquet and product-review Delta tables, refreshed on 13 October 2026. Invite history covers through 12 October and product-review events through 13 October, beyond the requested 30 September end date. No partial October data is included in either period.

Sends are production invite and reminder events partitioned by send date (_date); test-mode campaigns are excluded. All 638 current-period and 733 previous-period send events were unique on campaign, timestamp, send type, order and email.

Reviews use event_subtype = review_product, a populated product_rating, and review event date (ts_event). Attribution matches case-insensitive, trimmed order IDs against all-time production invite/reminder history, requiring a send on or before the review. No order mapped to multiple campaigns. Each of the 890 rated product-review records across June–September has a distinct product-review ID.

“Reviews” in campaign tables counts distinct reviewed orders, retaining the previous report’s convention. Each attributed order has exactly one product review in these periods, so reviewed-order and product-review totals coincide. Average ratings and rating distributions use product reviews once each, avoiding multiplication by reminder sends.

Review rate = reviewed orders attributed in the period ÷ invite sends in the period. Reminders are excluded from the denominator. Reviews can relate to earlier sends, so this is a period activity ratio rather than a conversion rate for the same send cohort. Unmatched product reviews may come from other channels or lack a matching order; no email fallback is used. No reviews means there is no average to calculate.
