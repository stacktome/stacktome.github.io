Correction to Previously Published June Figures

June review-attribution figures have been recalculated from the underlying Zeppelin data and corrected. The March–June 2026 report (published 9 Jul 2026) showed only 8 attributed June reviews in its per-campaign breakdown (invite-tp 7 · invite-closed 0 · invite-skus 0 · invite-loyal 1), explicitly flagged there as "partial" due to invite-to-review lag. Recomputing from Zeppelin today (with a full extra month of maturation) finds 30 attributed June reviews (invite-tp 22 · invite-closed 0 · invite-skus 1 · invite-loyal 7). The organic count (167), combined total (197), and negatives (32) in the previously published "Jun total" summary row were already correct and are unchanged — only the per-campaign attributed breakdown was understated in that report. This report uses the corrected per-campaign figures throughout.

Recomputation method: full invite history (all campaigns, Jan–Jul 2026) joined to June/July reviews by lowercased order_id, deduplicated one campaign per review via ROW_NUMBER() — the same method used for the March–June report, just re-run now that June's invite-to-review lag has largely settled. Organic reviews (no matching invite `order_id`, including reviews with no `order_id` at all) are included in every total below, per this report's brief.

Findings & Notes

  • July's attributed count (84) is still less "settled" than June's (30). June invites are 6–10 weeks old as of this report's generation date and have had time to fully convert; July invites are as little as 11 days old. Some of the July/June gap in review rate (0.46% vs 0.12%) is a genuine within-period difference, but part of it may narrow as more July reviews land in August. Re-check July's attributed total in the next report before treating the full 3.9× gap as durable.
  • June's review rate, even fully matured, is well below the March–May baseline. March–May blended review rate was ~0.68% (per the previous report); June (fully settled, corrected) is 0.12% — a real drop, not an artifact of this recomputation. Per-campaign: invite-tp fell from ~0.9% (Mar–May) to 0.17% (Jun); invite-loyal fell from ~0.4% to 0.06%. Worth investigating whether something changed in June (template, timing, list quality) independent of the July recovery.
  • Email-performance API can no longer answer historical questions beyond ~30 days back. This blocks any full-month open/click reconciliation for June going forward, and will block July once ~30 more days pass. If month-on-month email-engagement tracking matters, stats should be pulled and archived within the API's retention window rather than reconstructed later.
  • invite-skus (310) is the campaign to watch. It's the only campaign that grew invite volume in July (655 → 1,577) and its avg rating dropped sharply (5.0★ → 3.33★, n=6) with 3 of 6 attributed reviews negative — small sample, but worth revisiting alongside the March–June report's flagged concern about first-time Veg-SalCar-600 buyers lacking a purchase-count floor in this campaign's segment.
  • Subscription-cancellation complaints remain the single largest recurring theme, spanning both attributed and organic channels in both months — consistent with the March–June finding. This is a product/CX issue, not an invite-campaign issue, but it continues to be the most common driver of 1★–2★ reviews.
