5. Beautycos AT & DE — Inactive Accounts

Per the account requirement, both markets appear in every table above with zero activity. Details on why:

Austria (beautycos_at) — campaign 734, status inactive. Zero sends, all-time. Confirmed two ways: the campaign report API returns an empty stats: [] array with no date filter applied (i.e. not just this week — ever), and no _accountId=beautycos_at directory exists at all under /events/invites/email/_projectId=beautycos/ in storage. The campaign targets the native Trustpilot integration (no customInviteLink configured). Historical Trustpilot reviews do exist for this market outside the invite flow (25,784-row scan found an app_id=beautycos_at slice), but none fall in this week's window.

Germany (beautycos_de) — campaign 727, status inactive. Same empty-storage situation as Austria (no _accountId=beautycos_de directory), plus an active configuration bug: the campaign report API call fails outright with HTTP 400 Unknown_Sender — "noreply.invitations@beautycos.de". This means the sending domain/address is not provisioned, which would block sends even if the campaign were reactivated. Recommend flagging to whoever owns Beautycos email infrastructure — this needs a sender-domain fix, not just a campaign reactivation.

6. Findings & Recommendations

  • This is a launch-week snapshot. 13 of 15 markets sent their first-ever invite on Aug 3, 2026; only Denmark has meaningful history (since Jul 30). Review rate, attributed rating, and open/click percentages will all be noisy for at least 2–3 more weekly cycles — avoid over-reading single-digit sample sizes.
  • Denmark is the outlier by design, not by accident. It's the pilot market and carries 69% of this week's invite volume and 46% of reviews. Group-level averages are effectively Denmark-weighted until the other 12 markets scale up — consider reporting DK separately from the "other 12" median in future weeks, the way Den Sidste Flaske splits its markets.
  • Fix Germany's sender address. This is the one actionable infrastructure bug from this report — noreply.invitations@beautycos.de isn't a valid registered sender, blocking API-based reporting and presumably any future send attempts for that market.
  • Reconfirm Austria's status. Zero sends ever (not just paused) suggests this market may never have been fully launched, rather than recently deactivated — worth a business-side check on whether AT is still planned.
  • Organic signal (4.17★, N=35) looks healthier than the attributed signal (2.00★, N=4) this week — but both are too small to compare meaningfully yet. Once attributed volume builds, watch whether that gap persists (as it does for Den Sidste Flaske's DK market) or converges.
  • Data model is clean and consistent across all 13 active markets — identical contact struct schema in the invites Delta tables (no unionByName surprises), and customer_review is exclusively event_subtype='review_order' / event_source='trustpilot' group-wide, i.e. order-level Trustpilot reviews only, no product reviews. This matches every campaign's productInviteTarget: null configuration.
  • Naming gotcha for future reports: Belgium's storage _accountId is beautycos_be, not the pass-file/ST_ACCOUNT name beautycos_be_nl. Always translate between the two — see the Account Registry entry.
  • Czechia has zero Trustpilot reviews of any kind so far (not just this week — the full 2026 review scan found none), despite an active campaign since Aug 3. Smallest engagement signal of the 13 active markets; watch next week.
