6. Findings & Recommendations

  • Denmark's invite volume has been exactly 2,450 for three consecutive weeks. This is very unlikely to be coincidental organic order volume — it has the signature of a fixed daily-send or segment-size cap being hit consistently. Worth confirming with the account owner whether this is an intentional ceiling and, if so, whether it should be raised now that the market is established.
  • A serious organic Denmark review alleges the storefront itself is deceptive: "Don't get fooled by this China site! The product is not near what was promoted on Instagram! No address you can return the product to!!" (1★, Aug 21). This is a different and arguably more severe complaint shape than last week's counterfeit-product allegations — it questions the legitimacy of the storefront and the ability to return goods at all, not just product authenticity. Recommend routing to the same owner handling last week's counterfeit escalation, since it may be related.
  • A separate France organic review describes a damaged product plus a 3-month refund dispute that ultimately excluded shipping costs (1★, Aug 19). Different theme from the Denmark complaints above (fulfillment/refund-process, not product authenticity) — worth a look by whoever owns post-purchase support quality in France.
  • The other 12 markets' invite pullback from last week (−23.8%) fully reversed this week (+26.3%), landing back near the level from two weeks ago. Combined with Denmark's flat count, last week's dip now reads more like short-term noise than a sustained pullback — no action needed, but worth one more week of confirmation.
  • 5★ share has risen for a third consecutive week (69% → 79% → 85%) and the group review rate ticked up slightly (0.12% → 0.14%). Both are still small-sample signals but the direction has been consistent across all three reports so far.
  • Denmark logged 73 unsubscribes this window (1.5% of 4,718 sent) — the first time this has been surfaced in a report, since email performance detail wasn't broken out by unsub count before. No prior-week baseline exists for comparison; flagging so it can be tracked going forward rather than as a confirmed problem.
  • Czechia still has zero Trustpilot reviews of any kind (re-checked against the full 2026 history, not just this window), now across three full weeks of active sending (36 invites total). Smallest engagement signal of the 13 active markets; continue watching. Notably had the group's best open rate this week (70.6%) on very low volume (17 sends).
  • Six markets (UK, Poland, Finland, Ireland, Belgium, Czechia) had zero reviews this week despite active sending — a different set than last week's six (which included Spain, Netherlands but not UK or Belgium), consistent with still-ramping, low-volume markets rotating through quiet weeks rather than a data problem. Each is confirmed to have real invite/reminder activity in Section 2.
