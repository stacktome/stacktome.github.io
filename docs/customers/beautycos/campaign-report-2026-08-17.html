6. Findings & Recommendations

  • Two independent counterfeit-product allegations landed in the same week, in two different markets. Sweden's only attributed review this week (1★) and an organic Denmark review both describe products that don't match the authentic item they expected — the Sweden review specifically names a fragrance brand and describes physical inconsistencies versus two genuine bottles the customer already owned; the Denmark review describes a fragrance with no scent and a store that refused a refund. Two such reports in one week, across two markets, is worth escalating to whoever owns supplier/inventory quality assurance rather than treating as routine negative reviews.
  • Denmark's invite volume held exactly flat (2,450, unchanged from last week); the other 12 active markets' combined volume fell 23.8% (1,119 → 853). Worth confirming whether that's an intentional pullback now that each market's initial launch batch has gone out, or a segment/eligibility issue affecting the newer markets specifically.
  • Reminders are now live group-wide. Volume jumped from 300 (Denmark-only, last week) to 3,539 (all 13 active markets) as the 7-day delay elapsed for last week's launch cohort. This is expected and not a concern on its own.
  • Quality signal improved slightly week over week: 5★ share rose to 79% (33/42) from 69% (27/39), and reviews rated ≤2★ fell to 5 from 10 — though both weeks' samples remain small enough that this shouldn't be read as a firm trend yet.
  • Czechia still has zero Trustpilot reviews of any kind (re-checked against the full 2026 history, not just this window), now across two full weeks of active sending (33 invites total). Smallest engagement signal of the 13 active markets; continue watching.
  • Six markets (Czechia, Spain, Finland, Ireland, Netherlands, Poland) had zero reviews this week despite active sending. Consistent with still-ramping, low-volume markets rather than a data problem — each is confirmed to have real invite/reminder activity in Section 2.
  • Fix Germany's sender address and reconfirm Austria's launch status. Both carried over unresolved from last week's report; re-verified as still open this week, not stale findings.
