✔  Denmark’s unsubscribes halved, and 85% of reviews were 5★. Unsubscribes in the 13 established markets fell from 187 (as published last week) to 103 (still accruing), mostly in Denmark: 145 to 71, or 2.2% of tracked sends against 3.1%. Denmark’s fewer invites explain part of the fall, but the daily rate was also lower: 11–17 a day on full-volume days against 16–31 the week before. Norway received four 5★ reviews and the UK two.

The other 12 established markets sent 1,016 invites against 1,058 (−4.0%). Email engagement for those three days was correspondingly lower (Section 5).
ⓘ  New markets: no reviews this week. The 11 new markets sent 37 invites and 38 reminders (38 and 53 last week) and received no reviews. Since the Sep 21 launch they have received 6 post-launch reviews from 285 invites (Hungary 3, Slovenia 2, Portugal 1). Volumes remain very small. See Section 2.
ⓘ  Campaign status unchanged. Austria (734) and Germany (727) remain inactive. See Section 6.
