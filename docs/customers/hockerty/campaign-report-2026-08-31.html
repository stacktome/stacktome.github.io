7. Complaint Themes

📦
Packaging & Presentation
4 of 11 reviews this week (36.4%) — 4 attributed / 0 organic

The dominant theme this week — a sharp reversal from three straight reports where fit dominated and packaging was a minor footnote. Items arriving wrinkled, balled-up or in a plastic bag rather than a box, on suits, jackets and shirts across EN, FR and IT. All four are attributed (fresh or historical invites); none organic.

💻
Website & Ordering UX Issues New
3 of 11 reviews (27.3%) — 0 attributed / 3 organic

A theme not seen in prior reports: a mobile-ordering button-display bug, no way to save items for later without deleting them, and no credit-card payment option at checkout. All three are organic and none mention product fit at all — distinct from the account's usual product-quality complaints. Two of the three are also widget-converted orders (Section 5).

📏
Fit & Measurement Failures
2 of 11 reviews (18.2%) — 1 attributed / 1 organic

Down sharply from 73.3% last week and dominant for three straight reports before that. One severe case (suit far too large on every measurement) and one partial overlap with the packaging/CS-friction case above. Worth confirming this is genuine improvement rather than a sampling artifact given the smaller review volume overall this window.

Also present but below theme-card threshold: non-delivery/fulfillment (1, organic — package left outside building, no signature required) and CS/refund-process friction citing Spanish consumer law (1, organic — overlaps with the packaging theme above).
