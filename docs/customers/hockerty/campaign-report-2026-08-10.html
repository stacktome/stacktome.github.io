7. Complaint Themes

📏
Fit & Measurement Failures
9 of 23 reviews this week

Again the dominant theme, spanning suits, jackets, shoes, and dresses across EN, FR and DE on both accounts. Several cases explicitly note the customer provided professional or exact measurements and the result still didn't fit — including two wedding-critical orders (jacket and historical-campaign suit).

✂️
Quality & Manufacturing Defects
7 of 23 reviews this week

Second most common: transparent/thin fabric, scorch marks and wrong ironing, missing buttons, torn inseam stitching, missing pocket/wrong button count, and a repeat customer reporting a perceived quality decline across successive orders.

🌈
Color / Description Mismatches
3 of 23 reviews this week

A smaller but notable emerging theme, 2 of the 3 on Sumissura: products arriving in a completely different color than pictured, no swatches offered beforehand, and unresponsive returns processes when customers flagged the mismatch.

Also present but below theme-card threshold: unresolved/late delivery (2), undisclosed customs fees (1), and one severe fulfillment failure (3 remakes over 5 months, only partial store credit offered).
