5. Complaint Themes

✂
Fit & Measurement Failures
~28 of 80 negatives — every market, both accounts

The account's persistent, dominant issue: garments arriving larger or smaller than the measurements the customer submitted, on both menswear (Hockerty) and womenswear (Sumissura). Two independent Sumissura FR reviewers report the exact same inseam-length problem in the same month, and one explicitly recommends collecting the same body measurements from women that Hockerty already collects from men — a concrete, low-effort product fix.

📦
Delivery Delays & Wedding-Critical Timing
~18 of 80 negatives — concentrated in organic & FR

Multiple orders tied to a wedding date arrived after the event or days before it, including a costume delayed 15 days by an unresolved carrier dispute, a suit stuck in transit for two months with a DHL "incidencia," and a replacement order still open at month-end. Misrouted/misdelivered parcels and crumpled plastic-bag packaging recur as a secondary complaint on top of the delay itself.

💬
Customer Service & Refund Friction
~15 of 80 negatives — mostly organic

The sharpest reviews are about the resolution process, not the garment: refusal to refund because an order is "custom," no response to repeated emails, promised callbacks/escalations that never happened, and one reviewer explicitly identifying support replies as AI-generated with no human follow-through. This theme is concentrated almost entirely in organic (unattributed) reviews, suggesting these are customers who gave up on the invite/reminder flow entirely before writing publicly.
