7. Complaint Themes

📏
Fit & Measurement Failures
11 of 15 reviews this week (73.3%)

The dominant theme for a third straight report. Spans suits, jeans, shoes and coats across EN, FR, DE and IT on both accounts; several note that detailed or professional measurements were provided and the result still didn't fit.

📦
Non-Delivery / Fulfillment Failure
1 of 15 reviews — but severe

A single case, distinct from the usual late-delivery complaint: an order marked "delivered" to a pickup tailor whose address is permanently closed, with no customer-service response across multiple follow-ups. Worth investigating as a potential fulfillment-data or partner-network issue, not just a support-response gap.

✂️
Alteration & Refund Friction
3 of 15 reviews (overlaps with fit theme)

Smaller than the past two reports: a refused refund on undersized jeans, a months-long unresolved remake saga, and a Sumissura customer required to pay return shipping despite the stated fit guarantee.

Also present but below theme-card threshold: packaging/presentation complaints on otherwise-positive reviews (2, both Italian/French), and a fabric-weight/promo-timing complaint (1).
