7. Complaint Themes

📏
Fit & Measurement Failures
11 of 17 reviews this week (64.7%)

The dominant theme again, and a larger share than last week. Spans suits, shirts, and dresses across EN, FR, DE and ES on both accounts. Three cases are explicitly wedding-critical (two Hockerty, one Sumissura), and several note that professional or exact measurements were provided and the result still didn't fit.

✂️
Alteration & Returns Process Friction
5 of 17 reviews (overlaps with fit theme)

A distinct, compounding pattern this week: customers report the fix for a bad fit costs them money or time — self-funded alterations, return costs charged to the customer for the company's own sizing errors, a broken written refund promise, and an inaccessible alteration process on Sumissura DE.

📦
Fulfillment & Delivery Failures
2 of 17 reviews this week

Smaller than fit issues but still present: missing items with no customer-service response, and a delivery that arrived after the customer's event date, making the order unusable.

Also present but below theme-card threshold: checkout/promo-code UX friction (1, and notably the same review is one of this week's widget-driven conversions), and inconsistent quality across product categories on a single multi-year repeat order (1).
