7. Complaint Themes

📏
Fit & Measurement Failures
15 of 25 reviews this week

Still the dominant theme by a wide margin. Spans suits, trousers, and shirts across EN, FR, DE and both accounts: measurements re-transcribed incorrectly on a replacement order, perfect-fit guarantees not honored, repeat remakes still not fitting (including two separate wedding-suit cases), and updated measurements not being applied to new orders.

🧱
Fabric & Construction Quality
5 of 25 reviews this week

Fabric described as "shiny plastic," too thin even on the premium option, disappointing on a custom dress, and weak/single-line pocket stitching expected to deteriorate. Distinct from the fit theme — these are material and workmanship complaints on garments that otherwise fit as ordered.

🚫
Refund / Returns Refused
4 of 25 reviews this week

Refunds declined via a "custom product" technicality even when the wrong item was delivered, a perfect-fit guarantee that wasn't honored with a return, and a wedding case with three failed remakes still awaiting resolution. Continued reputational risk alongside the fit theme.
