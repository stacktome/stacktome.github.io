7. Complaint Themes

✂
Fit, sizing and accuracy
7 of 9 negatives

Garments that were too small or too large, a collar measured larger than ordered, a relaxed fit that arrived as a standard cut, and trousers that local tailoring could not correct.

↩
Alterations and remakes
4 of 9 negatives

A partner alteration arrangement the partner knew nothing about, a remeasure-and-photos process that ended in a tailor referral, a remade suit that fitted worse, and a ticket closed before an alteration receipt was uploaded. The 705 reviewer had already contacted support three times.

🚚
Delivery timing and event deadlines
1 of 9 negatives

One reviewer received the order a couple of days after the committed date and missed the event; they advise others to expect a few extra days.

✔
Quality and fabric
3 of 9 negatives

Frayed, cheap-looking fabric, a chino fabric judged poor, and a shiny material that looked less premium than the website photos. One 5★ reviewer also warns that non-premium shirt fabrics shrank despite hand washing.

🖥
Website and ordering
4 of 9 negatives

Website photos that did not match the delivered product (two reviews), an unclear gift-card field at checkout, and a body-measurement profile overwritten by a support-requested remeasure.

Themes overlap, so counts do not add to 9. Confirm each case history before drawing conclusions from review text alone.
