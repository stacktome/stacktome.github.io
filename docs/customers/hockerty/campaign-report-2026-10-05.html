7. Complaint Themes

✂
Fit, sizing and accuracy
9 of 17 negatives

Garments or shoes that did not fit, were made in the wrong size or specification, or needed remakes. Reviewers also point to measurement-entry limits and automatically calculated measurements.

↩
Alterations, refunds and returns
5 of 17 negatives

Refund refusals, a 30% offer toward a new item, 25% tailoring reimbursement, a ruined alteration and return-shipping cost. All three Hockerty EN tickets (705) negatives had already contacted support.

🚚
Delivery timing and event deadlines
4 of 17 negatives

Two reviews describe garments arriving or being remade too late for a wedding, one parcel was returned to the carrier days before a wedding, and one reviewer suggests stating lead times up front.

✔
Quality control and presentation
3 of 17 negatives

Creased garments in fabric bags, a pulled thread, a missing composition label and off-specification details. Packaging also drew comments in three 4–5★ reviews.

🖥
Website and ordering
3 of 17 negatives

iPhone editing resets, a promotion that took several emails to resolve, an unintuitive checkout and limited shirt-fabric choice. A 4★ review also called the AI assistant unhelpful.

Themes overlap, so counts do not add to 17. Confirm each case history before drawing conclusions from review text alone.
