Ratings improved and negative reviews fell. 53 reviews were received (56 last week) and the average rose to 4.57★ from 4.36★; 49 of 53 reviews (92.5%) were 4–5★ versus 47 of 56 (83.9%). Negatives fell from 9 to 4 (7.5% of reviews, from 16.1%). All four concerned Hockerty and none was invite-linked: three organic 1★ reviews and one widget-generated 3★. Two 1★ reviews describe orders stalled at quality control since Oct 19 ahead of events, with emails left unanswered; the other reports incorrect measurements and a refused refund (section 7).
Invite volume fell while reminders rose sharply. Daily invites were 17–55 this week (highest on Oct 28), after 29–69 the previous week. Weekly invites fell 13.9% (281 to 242), driven by the pre-sales campaign (820): it sent 14 invites after 61 in its first week. Excluding pre-sales, invites rose 3.6% (220 to 228): Hockerty main campaigns rose 126 to 130 (IT 9 to 22, ES 3 to 11, EN 48 to 56, while FR fell 31 to 16 and DE 35 to 25), and Hockerty ticket campaigns rose 76 to 79. Reminders rose 42.6% (136 to 194), mostly in the main Hockerty campaigns (77 to 117) and the Hockerty ticket campaigns (46 to 65), so total emails rose 4.6%.
The Review Rate fell back as invite-linked reviews dropped. 11 reviews were invite-linked (24 last week), so the Review Rate fell to 4.55% from 8.54%; excluding pre-sales it was 4.82% (11 / 228) against 10.0% (22 / 220) last week, when Hockerty tickets-EN alone produced 8 reviews (5 this week). Widget-generated reviews rose to 32 (20), alongside 98 widget actions versus 85. Customers often review a week or more after being invited, so the higher reminder volume may convert in next week’s figures.
