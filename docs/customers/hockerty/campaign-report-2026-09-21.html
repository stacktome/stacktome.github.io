Total invites fell from 663 to 249 (−62.4%), and the drop is broad-based rather than one campaign: every Hockerty main-language campaign fell 68–81% (EN 237→57, FR 96→31, DE 98→19, ES 29→6, IT 32→9), and the tickets programme's invite volume nearly halved (142→75) even as its reminder volume grew sharply (6→130) working through last week's backlog.
