Includes invite-linked, widget-generated and organic reviews. A negative is a review rated 1–3★. Last week: 53 reviews, 4.57★, 4 negatives (7.5%).

Review volume rose by almost half, but the average rating eased. 78 reviews were received (53 last week) and the average was 4.40★ versus 4.57★; 67 of 78 reviews (85.9%) were 4–5★ versus 49 of 53 (92.5%). Negatives rose from 4 to 11 (14.1%); ten concerned Hockerty and one Sumissura. Three were invite-linked, three widget-generated and five organic. One of the seven 1★ reviews carries a positive comment (“no problems”), so the rating may be a mistake. The rest involve a vest delivered after the customer’s event with a return refused, a long-unresolved complaint, fit and quality, costs beyond the price, and checkout confusion over promotion codes (section 7).
Invite volume rose 34%, almost all in the main Hockerty campaigns. Daily invites were 17–65 (highest on Nov 3), after 17–55 the previous week. Weekly invites rose from 242 to 325: main Hockerty campaigns 130 to 222, led by EN (56 to 113, with daily invites stepping up from about 9 to 12–23 from Nov 2), FR (16 to 40) and DE (25 to 39), while Hockerty ticket campaigns fell from 79 to 66 and pre-sales (820) held steady at 15 (14). Reminders fell 4.6% (194 to 185), so total emails rose 17.0%. The rise follows order intake: Hockerty recorded 2,523 orders this week against 1,685 the week before, 1,271 the week before that and 701 in mid-October (Sumissura 283, from 199), and invites generally follow a few weeks after orders, so volume may keep rising.
The Review Rate recovered. 24 reviews were invite-linked (11 last week), lifting the Review Rate to 7.38% from 4.55% even though invites rose 34.3%; excluding pre-sales it was 7.74% (24 / 310) versus 4.82% (11 / 228). Ticket campaigns (excluding pre-sales) converted 17.1% (13 reviews / 76 invites) against 4.7% for the main campaigns (11 / 234). Widget-generated reviews were 37 (32), alongside 111 widget actions versus 98.
Email engagement: 507 tracked sends, 316 unique opens (62.3%), 6 unique clicks and 3 unsubscribes, compared with 439 tracked sends, 62.2% opens, 10 unique clicks and 3 unsubscribes the previous week. The open rate held overall, but the main Hockerty campaigns slipped from 60.7% to 56.0% (EN 61.2% to 55.7%, FR 59.1% to 51.0%) while ticket campaigns opened at 73.8%. The latest emails of the week have had less time to collect opens, so the open rate may rise slightly.
