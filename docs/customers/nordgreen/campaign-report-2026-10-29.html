Rating is flat at 3.85★, but the mix behind it has changed. The year-to-date average was 4.16★ in July; the last four months (Jul–Oct) have run 3.65–4.20★ month to month. Reviews are driven down by volume: order volume has fallen from an August peak (1,043 orders) to 818 in September and 609 in October to date, and invites have followed (1,026 in the window vs 1,444 in the prior 60 days).

All 6 negative reviews in the window are from UK customers, and 4 of the 6 are about unexpected post-purchase charges (import duty / courier fees on orders shipped from Denmark, and return deductions) — not product quality. See Negative Review Themes and Action Items.

The invite-de (456) campaign has almost stopped sending — 257 invites in June, 41 in August, 16 in October — while Germany-shipped orders held at roughly 150–245 a month. That is a pipeline issue, not a demand issue (details in Campaign Performance).

Negative Review Themes

6 reviews rated ≤2★ in the window (1×2★, 5×1★), all read individually. All 6 are from UK (gb) delivery addresses. This window is small, so themes are counted by hand rather than by keyword match; they are mutually exclusive here.

💷
Unexpected UK Duty & Fees
3 of 6 negative reviews (50%)

Customers charged import duty and courier handling fees after delivery — £56 (FedEx, billed a week after receipt, too late to return), £43.23 (billed three weeks after a return), £47.88 (“misleading advertising”: free shipping advertised, not stated that goods ship from Denmark with import taxes due). Two reviewers say the charge wasn't disclosed at checkout.

↩️
Return Costs
1 of 6 negative reviews (17%)

A customer objects to ~£10 being deducted from the refund on top of paying return postage. Combined with the duty reviews, 4 of 6 negatives are about money charged after the purchase decision.

💬
Customer Service Unresponsive

A customer trying to cancel an order over 3–4 days got no reply by email or Instagram, then had a failed delivery with no help. Notes the support address listed on the India site does not exist.

Action Items

  • 🔴
    UK customers are being surprised by post-purchase charges: import duty/courier fees (3 negatives) and return deductions (1), so 4 of the 6 negatives; all 6 negatives are UK. UK is the largest review market (17 of 27 reviews) and the source of every negative in the window. Options: disclose “ships from Denmark, import duty may apply” at checkout and in the free-shipping banner, or move UK orders to a duty-paid (DDP) service.
  • 🔴
    invite-de (456) volume is down ~94% since June (257 invites to 16 in October) while German orders are steady at ~150–245 a month. This is not demand. Check the campaign's segment/filters and send schedule for a change since July; its few recent invites converted well (3 reviews, all 5★, from 36 invites), so restoring volume has upside. Related to the earlier invite-dk concern, which now looks demand-driven (see Campaign Performance).
  • 🟡
    Order volume is declining from the August peak (1,043 → 818 → 609 in October to date). Reviews follow invite volume, so expect fewer reviews unless orders recover. Worth confirming with the client whether this is seasonal or a marketing/traffic change.
  • 🟡
    13 of 27 reviews cannot be matched to a campaign (9 with no order_id, 4 with no invite on record), and they average 3.23★ vs 4.43★ for matched reviews. Because they are unmatched they cannot be followed up with the customer or credited to a campaign, and per-campaign figures understate every campaign's true review rate. Worth checking whether the review link could carry the order reference automatically.
  • 🟡
    Customer-service responsiveness appears in one negative review (unanswered cancellation request, support address on the India site not working). One review is an anecdote, but the July report also flagged slow/AI-routed support. Worth checking the India-site contact address.
  • 🟡
    Still no support-ticket data source for this account. The negative reviews above cannot be checked for whether the customer had contacted support first. Confirm whether ticket data can be piped into the pipeline for the next report.
  • 🟢
    Email performance improved: 67.3% open rate (58.7% in July) and 3.9% click rate over the last 30 days, with unsubscribes steady at 3.6%.
  • 🟢
    Warranty and customer service are now praised in 5★ reviews (all five highlights above cite fast warranty replacements or helpful support), a change from the July report where warranty policy was a leading negative theme. Durability complaints fell from 39% of negatives YTD to 1 of 6 in this window.
