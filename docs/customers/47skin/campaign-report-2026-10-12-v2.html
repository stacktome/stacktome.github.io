How reviews are attributed. A review counts only if its order reference is the same order that campaign 811, 812 or 813 emailed (invite or reminder), and the review was written after that email. Matching on the customer alone (same email address, no matching order) is not accepted: one review matched only that way and is excluded. Review Rate = attributed reviews ÷ invites sent in the week. The invitation link cannot be traced to the review, so this shows the order was emailed before it was reviewed (2–47 hours earlier), not that the email caused the review. Reviews without an order reference, reviews of other orders and product reviews are excluded.

What changed. The campaigns sent 1,965 invites in their first 7 days, led by Recent Purchases (734), Closed Tickets (670) and Loyal Purchases (561), plus 350 reminders. Fourteen reviews are attributed to the campaigns (0.71% of invites): 10 from Closed Tickets (813), 3 from Loyal Purchases (812) and 1 from Recent Purchases (811). Twelve are 4–5★ and the average is 4.50★. Closed Tickets produced the only two low ratings (1★ and 3★), both about problems that were still open.
Two invited customers had unresolved problems. A customer with a ~£200 express order that DPD returned to sender (1★) and a customer still waiting for a subscription refund (3★) both reviewed within hours of receiving a Positive Closed Tickets (813) invite. Both reviews say the issue was still open. See Section 4.
Email engagement: 2,291 tracked sends (emails covered by the email platform’s engagement reporting), 1,332 unique opens (58.1%), 85 unique clicks (3.7%) and 16 unsubscribes (0.7%). The latest days of the week continue to collect opens and clicks, so these figures are provisional. Reviews are included through Oct 11; later ones are in next week’s report.

3. Campaign Breakdown

Recent Purchases (811)
Largest campaign: 734 invites and 39 reminders. It hit its 150-a-day sending limit on Oct 7 and Oct 8, then ran at 60–101 a day. One attributed review (0.14% review rate), 5★, written on Oct 11 about four hours after its invite. Opens 60.0%, clicks 2.3% (the lowest click rate of the three), 5 unsubscribes. Two invite templates are being tested: the brand-style invite and the Trustpilot-style invite (details below).
Positive Closed Tickets (813)
670 invites and 311 reminders (72 hours after the invite), with the best engagement: 58.5% opens, 5.8% clicks. Ten attributed reviews (1.49% review rate, avg 4.30★): eight after the invite and two after the reminder. Eight are 4–5★ and praise quick, helpful customer service. Two are low (1★ and 3★), both from customers whose issue was still open. Invites were 36–90 a day apart from Oct 9, when 282 went out, mostly in two bursts around 10:00–12:00 and 15:00–17:00 UTC; the cause was not checked. 9 of the week’s 16 unsubscribes.
Loyal Purchases (812)
561 invites and no reminders yet (its reminders are set for seven days after the invite, so they begin on Oct 12). It hit its 150-a-day limit on Oct 7, then fell to 33–94 a day. Three attributed reviews (0.53%), all 5★. Opens 55.0%, clicks 1.9%, 2 unsubscribes.
Invite template test (811 and 812)
The brand-style invite (about 639 sent) had 54.4% opens and 2.8% clicks; the Trustpilot-style invite (about 656 sent) had 61.0% opens and 1.5% clicks. Opens favour the Trustpilot style and clicks the brand style. With only 4 attributed reviews between the two campaigns, review results cannot yet separate them.

Reading the A/B results. The Trustpilot-style template (invite-tp) is opened more (61.0% vs 54.4%) while the brand template gets more clicks (2.8% vs 1.5%); the two subject lines on 811/812 are close on opens and clicks (58.6% vs 56.8% opens). In 813, the “recent enquiry” subject line has more clicks (8.4% vs 5.0%) but slightly fewer opens (58.9% vs 61.2%), and 7 of its 8 attributed reviews sit under it, including the 3★ and 1★ reviews. Sample sizes are very small: each variation has 130–366 invites and 0–7 attributed reviews, reminder variations have 5–161 sends, and gaps of a few percentage points are within normal variation. No variation can be declared a winner yet; review rates are not yet reliable for choosing between them. Reminders are not given a review rate because they follow invites already counted.
