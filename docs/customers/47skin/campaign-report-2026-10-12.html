What changed. The campaigns sent 1,965 invites in their first 7 days, led by Recent Purchases (734), Closed Tickets (670) and Loyal Purchases (561), plus 350 reminders. Service reviews fell from 45 to 35, but the average rating rose to 4.69★ and 1–2★ reviews halved from 4 to 2. Fifteen of the 35 reviews came from customers who had been emailed first; 10 of those 15 came from the Closed Tickets campaign (813), which also produced both of its low-rated invite-linked reviews. Last week’s negatives were mostly about the product not working and subscriptions; this week they were a delivery failure, a product-efficacy comment and a subscription refund still pending.
Two invited customers had unresolved problems. A customer with a ~£200 express order that DPD returned to sender (1★) and a customer still waiting for a subscription refund (3★) both reviewed within a day of receiving a Positive Closed Tickets (813) invite. Both reviews say the issue was still open. See Section 4.
Email engagement: 2,291 tracked sends (emails covered by the email platform’s engagement reporting), 1,332 unique opens (58.1%), 85 unique clicks (3.7%) and 16 unsubscribes (0.7%). The latest days of the week continue to collect opens and clicks, so these figures are provisional. Reviews are included through Oct 11; later ones are in next week’s report.

3. Campaign Breakdown

Recent Purchases (811)
Largest campaign: 734 invites and 39 reminders. It hit its 150-a-day sending limit on Oct 7 and Oct 8, then ran at 60–101 a day. Two linked reviews (0.27% review rate), both 5★. Opens 60.0%, clicks 2.3% (the lowest click rate of the three), 5 unsubscribes. Two invite templates are being tested: the brand-style invite and the Trustpilot-style invite (details below).
Positive Closed Tickets (813)
670 invites and 311 reminders (72 hours after the invite), with the best engagement: 58.5% opens, 5.8% clicks. Ten linked reviews (1.49% review rate, avg 4.30★): eight after the invite and two after the reminder. Eight are 4–5★ and praise quick, helpful customer service. Two are low (1★ and 3★), both from customers whose issue was still open. Invites were 36–90 a day apart from Oct 9, when 282 went out, mostly in two bursts around 10:00–12:00 and 15:00–17:00 UTC; the cause was not checked. 9 of the week’s 16 unsubscribes.
Loyal Purchases (812)
561 invites and no reminders yet (its reminders are set for seven days after the invite, so they begin on Oct 12). It hit its 150-a-day limit on Oct 7, then fell to 33–94 a day. Three linked reviews (0.53%), all 5★. Opens 55.0%, clicks 1.9%, 2 unsubscribes.
Invite template test (811 and 812)
The brand-style invite (about 639 sent) had 54.4% opens and 2.8% clicks; the Trustpilot-style invite (about 656 sent) had 61.0% opens and 1.5% clicks. Opens favour the Trustpilot style and clicks the brand style. With 5 linked reviews between the two campaigns, review results cannot yet separate them.
