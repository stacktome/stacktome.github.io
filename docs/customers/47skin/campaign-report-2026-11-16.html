How reviews are attributed. A review counts only if its order reference is the same order that campaign 811, 812, 813 (UK) or 817 (USA) emailed (invite or reminder), and the review was written after that email. It is assigned to the account and campaign that sent that email. Reviews on the shared Trustpilot profile are counted once each, and no order reference was emailed by both accounts. Matching on the customer alone (same email address, no matching order) is not accepted. Three reviews matched a campaign customer only that way this week and are not counted; another 30 reviews on the profile had no link to a campaign order and are also excluded (41 reviews on the profile in total). Review Rate = attributed reviews ÷ invites sent in the week. The invitation link cannot be traced to the review, so this shows the order was emailed before it was reviewed (2–30 hours earlier), not that the email caused the review. Reviews without an order reference, reviews of other orders and product reviews are excluded.

Reading the A/B results. The 811/812 invite test and the USA reminder subject test have ended, with the brand-style invite, the “We’d love to hear about your experience” subject and the “Regarding your recent inquiry” reminder kept.
