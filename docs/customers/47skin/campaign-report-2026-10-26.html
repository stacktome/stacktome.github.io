How reviews are attributed. A review counts only if its order reference is the same order that campaign 811, 812, 813 (UK) or 817 (USA) emailed (invite or reminder), and the review was written after that email; it is assigned to the account and campaign that sent that email. Reviews on the shared Trustpilot profile are counted once each, and no order reference was emailed by both accounts. Matching on the customer alone (same email address, no matching order) is not accepted; no review matched only that way this week. Review Rate = attributed reviews ÷ invites sent in the week. The invitation link cannot be traced to the review, so this shows the order was emailed before it was reviewed (2–178 hours earlier), not that the email caused the review. Reviews without an order reference, reviews of other orders and product reviews are excluded.

Reading the A/B results. The UK reminder subject-line test ended on Oct 19: “30 seconds to tell us how we did?” was last sent around midday on Oct 19 (37 UK reminders this week) and UK reminders now use only “Regarding your recent order/enquiry”, the subject line that led on opens in the first two weeks; it carries 11 of the 12 reviews that followed a reminder this week. On 811/812 invites the brand-style template was slightly ahead this week (55.7% vs 54.0% opens, 2.9% vs 1.2% clicks), after the Trustpilot style led on opens in the first two weeks, and the two subject lines were close on opens (55.7% vs 54.3%). In 813 the two invite subject lines performed almost identically (65.1% vs 64.2% opens, 7.7% vs 7.0% clicks, 4 reviews each). The USA reminder test (817) continues: “Regarding your recent inquiry” was opened more (63.9% vs 59.6%) but “30 seconds” had all the clicks (4.3% vs 0.0%). Sample sizes are still small: invite variations have 21–63 sends on 811/812, 234–240 on 813 and 14–27 on 817, with 0–4 attributed reviews each, and gaps of a few percentage points are within normal variation. No invite variation can be declared a winner yet; review rates are not yet reliable for choosing between them. Reminders are not given a review rate because they follow invites already counted.
