How reviews are attributed. A review counts only if its order reference is the same order that campaign 811, 812, 813 (UK) or 817 (USA) emailed (invite or reminder), and the review was written after that email; it is assigned to the account and campaign that sent that email. Reviews on the shared Trustpilot profile are counted once each, and no order reference was emailed by both accounts. Matching on the customer alone (same email address, no matching order) is not accepted; four reviews matched a campaign customer only that way this week and are not counted. Review Rate = attributed reviews ÷ invites sent in the week. The invitation link cannot be traced to the review, so this shows the order was emailed before it was reviewed (2–24 hours earlier), not that the email caused the review. Reviews without an order reference, reviews of other orders and product reviews are excluded.

What changed. Sending volume rose sharply. Invites went from 619 to 1,163: Recent Purchases (811) from 224 to 447 and Loyal Purchases (812) from 127 to 439 (both sent 150 invites on Thu Nov 5, their daily limit, after 16–38 a day on Nov 2–4), USA Closed Tickets (817) from 10 to 56 (36 of them on Nov 2), while UK Closed Tickets (813) fell from 258 to 221. Reminders rose from 537 to 847 across all four campaigns (811: 189 to 251; 812: 106 to 248; 813: 232 to 282; 817: 10 to 66). Twelve reviews are attributed to the campaigns (1.03% of invites; the rate eased because invites grew faster than reviews, which arrive days after an invite): eleven UK (six from Closed Tickets 813, four from Loyal Purchases 812, one from Recent Purchases 811) and the first from USA Closed Tickets. Ten are 5★, one is 4★ and one is 1★, so the average rose slightly to 4.58★; there were no 2★ or 3★ reviews.
Closed Tickets (813) feedback stayed positive, and USA is reviewing again
Five of the six attributed 813 reviews are 5★ and praise helpful service, four of them singling out how fast the response was (“their response is super quick”, a query answered quickly and efficiently); all six came after a reminder. The one negative review is from a person who emailed about working together and received review requests instead of a reply (Section 4). USA Closed Tickets (817) received an attributed review, a 5★ calling the customer service great, after none in the previous two weeks (three in its launch week).
Email engagement: 1,996 tracked sends across both accounts (emails covered by the email platform’s engagement reporting), 1,115 unique opens (55.9%), 34 unique clicks (1.7%) and 3 unsubscribes (0.2%): UK 1,874 tracked sends (55.8% opens, 1.8% clicks, 3 unsubscribes) and USA 122 (57.4%, 0.0%, 0). This compares with 1,144 tracked sends, 57.9% opens, 3.5% clicks and 12 unsubscribes last week (UK 1,124: 58.1%, 3.6%, 12; USA 20: 45.0%, 0.0%, 0). Opens eased by 2 points and clicks halved, partly because Recent and Loyal Purchases (811/812), which have very low click rates (0.6%), made up 1,382 of this week’s 1,996 tracked sends (about 69%, against 56% last week), while Closed Tickets (813) clicks fell from 7.1% to 5.3%. Unsubscribes fell from 12 (1.0% of tracked sends) to 3 (0.2%). The latest days of the week continue to collect opens and clicks, so these figures are provisional.

Reading the A/B results. UK reminders use only “Regarding your recent order/enquiry”, so there is no UK reminder subject test. On 811/812 invites the brand-style template led on opens (53.7% vs 51.7%) and clicks (5 vs 2 clicks), and “We’d love to hear about your experience” led on opens over “How did we do?” (55.0% vs 50.6%), the reverse of last week on both counts (Trustpilot style 57.9% vs 52.6%; “How did we do?” 57.5% vs 52.7%). In 813 the two invite subject lines performed similarly (53.3% vs 50.9% opens, 5.6% vs 5.4% clicks) and neither produced an attributed review; all six 813 reviews followed the reminder. The USA tests (817) saw 27–37 sends per variation, with the reminder “Regarding your recent inquiry” opening at 75.7% against 37.9% for “30 seconds to tell us how we did?”. Sample sizes are still small: invite variations have 98–127 sends on 811/812, 109–112 on 813 and 27–29 on 817, with 0–2 attributed reviews each, and gaps of several percentage points are within normal variation. The leader on 811/812 switched between weeks, so no variation can be declared a winner yet. Reminders are not given a review rate because they follow invites already counted.
