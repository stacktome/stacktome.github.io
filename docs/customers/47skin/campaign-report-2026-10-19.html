1. Executive Summary

Second week of sending, now covering both accounts. The three 47skin UK review campaigns (Recent Purchases 811, Loyal Purchases 812, Positive Closed Tickets 813) completed their second week; Oct 5–11 was the launch week (sending began Oct 5 at about 14:00 UTC) and is the comparison week below. Reviews often arrive days after an invite, and this week most attributed reviews came after a reminder to customers first invited the week before, so the Review Rate is a weekly indicator rather than a conversion rate for this week’s invites.

How reviews are attributed. A review counts only if its order reference is the same order that campaign 811, 812, 813 (UK) or 817 (USA) emailed (invite or reminder), and the review was written after that email; it is assigned to the account and campaign that sent that email. Reviews on the shared Trustpilot profile are counted once each, and no order reference was emailed by both accounts. Matching on the customer alone (same email address, no matching order) is not accepted: three reviews matched only that way and are excluded. Review Rate = attributed reviews ÷ invites sent in the week. The invitation link cannot be traced to the review, so this shows the order was emailed before it was reviewed (2–51 hours earlier), not that the email caused the review. Reviews without an order reference, reviews of other orders and product reviews are excluded.

What changed. Invites fell from 1,965 to 1,418 as Recent Purchases (734 to 357) and Loyal Purchases (561 to 159) slowed after their launch-week peak, while Closed Tickets rose (670 to 902). Reminders rose from 350 to 2,520 as the launch week’s invites became due (seven days after the invite for Recent and Loyal Purchases, 72 hours for Closed Tickets). The USA Closed Tickets campaign (817) added 165 invites and 69 reminders in its first week. Thirty-four reviews are attributed to the campaigns (2.15% of invites): 31 UK (20 from Closed Tickets 813, 7 from Loyal Purchases 812, 4 from Recent Purchases 811) and 3 USA (817); 22 of them came after a reminder. 3. Campaign Breakdown

UK · Recent Purchases (811)
357 invites (734 in the launch week) and 783 reminders. Invites ran at 16–70 a day, well below the 150-a-day limit it reached in the launch week. Four attributed reviews (1.12% review rate, avg 3.25★): one after the invite (4★) and three after a reminder (4★, 3★ and a 2★ from a subscriber whose bottle arrived half full). Opens 60.8%, clicks 3.0%, 11 unsubscribes. Its reminders now mostly go out seven days after the invite, as configured. Two invite templates are being tested: the brand-style invite and the Trustpilot-style invite (details below).
UK · Positive Closed Tickets (813)
902 invites and 1,084 reminders (72 hours after the invite), again with the best engagement: 58.8% opens, 9.7% clicks. Twenty attributed reviews (2.22% review rate, avg 3.75★): eight after the invite and twelve after the reminder. Fourteen are 4–5★ and praise quick, helpful customer service and fast replacements for damaged items. Six are 1–2★, three of them from customers who say they cancelled a subscription but were still charged or sent another order; one (slow shipping) is a 47skin USA store order that this UK campaign emailed, so it is counted here. Invites were 39–66 a day apart from Oct 12 (467) and Oct 15 (178); the cause of these peaks was not checked. 29 of the week’s 43 unsubscribes.
UK · Loyal Purchases (812)
159 invites (561 in the launch week) and its first 653 reminders, sent seven days after the invite. Invites ran at 9–32 a day. Seven attributed reviews (4.40% review rate, avg 4.86★), six of them after a reminder: six 5★ and one 4★. Opens 57.8%, clicks 2.2%, 3 unsubscribes. Two reviewers note that it is hard to use all of the product from pump jars and nozzle bottles.
USA · Positive Closed Tickets (817)
First week of sending, from Oct 13 at about 19:00 UTC: 165 invites and 69 reminders (72 hours after the invite). Invites came mainly on Oct 14 (57) and Oct 16 (77), with 5–10 on other days. Opens 65.8%, the highest of the four campaigns, clicks 4.0%, 3 unsubscribes. Three attributed reviews (1.82% review rate, avg 3.67★): two 5★ (a parent whose daughter’s skin cleared, and a long-term user praising responsive service) and one 1★ saying the product had no effect.

Reading the A/B results. The Trustpilot-style template (invite-tp) is again opened more (60.2% vs 55.3%), and clicks are now close (3.9% vs 3.5%), after the brand template led on clicks in the launch week. On 811/812 invites the “We’d love to hear about your experience” subject line had more opens (60.8% vs 54.7%), reversing last week’s small gap. In 813, the “recent enquiry” subject line again has more clicks (12.8% vs 8.7%) with similar opens (61.3% vs 62.9%), and 5 of the 8 reviews after an 813 invite sit under it, including two 1★ reviews. In the USA campaign (817), the “support team” subject line was opened far more (79.5% vs 58.1%) and carries both invite-attributed reviews, the opposite of UK 813 on opens, though each USA variation has fewer than 90 sends. On reminders, the “Regarding your recent order/enquiry/inquiry” subject lines were opened more than “30 seconds to tell us how we did?” in all six template and campaign pairs, including 817 (60.5–70.2% vs 51.1–57.1%), and carry 17 of the 22 reviews that followed a reminder. Sample sizes are still small: invite variations have 34–97 sends on 811/812, 432–470 on 813 and 76–89 on 817, with 0–5 attributed reviews each, and gaps of a few percentage points are within normal variation. No variation can be declared a winner yet; review rates are not yet reliable for choosing between them. Reminders are not given a review rate because they follow invites already counted.
